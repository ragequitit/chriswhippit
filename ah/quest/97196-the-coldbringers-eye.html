Quest text

There is a servant of the Lich King that makes his home deep in the Death's Head quilboar lair known as Razorfen Downs. It is known to us that he makes use of a scrying orb to observe and direct the movements of his minions elsewhere. With this artifact, we'd be able to hunt down the covert agents of the Scourge and put an end to them and the threat they pose to Kalimdor forever. Go into Razorfen Downs to the lair of Amnennar the Coldbringer and retrieve the orb known as the Coldbringer's Eye for me.
