Borrowed Blades

Quest · Riverglades · Horde
Level ?Required –XP 2850

Quest text

With the human presence to the south and Twilight to the west, the battle for resources has become difficult. But why gather resources amongst our enemies when their blades will suffice? Follow the river south to the mill. When you hear the whistling of woodworkers among the trees, tread lightly. When the opportunity presents itself, strike hard and fast. I'll stay behind to keep the forge running hot. Gur'dok will break down the blades while I fashion weaponry forged of their own materials.
