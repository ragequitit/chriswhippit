Burrow Beetles

Quest · Mount Hyjal · Horde & Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 3000

Quest text

Burrow beetles! They're a bane... and now they dig in our den more than their own. We beat them back and swing clubs when we see them skulk by, but they are quick, and to hide in the dark is their shield. If you spot them as you climb our den, squash them! We will take their crushed shells and make a meal, so it is not all bad.
