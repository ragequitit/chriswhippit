There exists a troll witch doctor that goes by the name of Zum'rah. I have history with this particular troll, and I'd like you to kill him for me. Go to the Sandfury stronghold of Zul'Farrak, deep in the deserts of Tanaris. Find Zum'rah inside and destroy him. Bring his head to me--intact--and I'll see that you are fairly compensated.
