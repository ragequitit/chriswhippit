Coyote Huntin'

Quest · Riverglades · Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 2850

Quest text

There's a pack of coyotes livin' in a den west of here. We can see 'em sometimes from across the river. They used to hunt fish on the shore, but I guess the murlocs pushed 'em out of their usual feeding ground, and so they've taken to eating horses. The leader of the coyote pack's a bigger one with dark fur. We'd nicknamed him "Sootfur". I reckon if you kill him, the rest'll scatter.
