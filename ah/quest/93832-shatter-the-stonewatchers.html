Quest text

High slopes hold stone wings. Rock-beasts. Half-live, half-dead. Foul hands carve them. Do not trust their still pose. These eyes wait for one wrong step. Our young do not know the risk. One paw too near, and stone wakes with claw and fang. Go, smash these cursed forms. Break their wings, crush their shards, and let the mountain breathe free.
