Spoils of War

Quest · Wetlands · Alliance
Level ~26Required –XP 1750Money 14s

Quest text

This harbor was first built by the Old Horde as a staging ground for their naval invasion of Hillsbrad. Completely circumvented Lordaeron's defenses! So many lives lost... What's worse is that fleet was built with the very resources they stole from Khaz Modan! Plenty of it remains in the harbor after Admiral Proudmoore routed the brutes and built this place for the Alliance. We'll need it to bolster the town's defenses with the Dragonmaw on the move.
