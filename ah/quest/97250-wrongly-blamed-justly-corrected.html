Wrongly Blamed, Justly Corrected

Quest · The Barrens · Horde · Elite
Level ~18Required –XP 2300

Quest text

Traveler, have you plans to head toward Dustwallow Marsh? I have heard mutterings that a few human soldiers have taken a stand at its border and are toppling the landmarks left behind by our tauren pathfinders. Rumor has it that a favored tavern has burned to the ground, and in typical Alliance fashion, they immediately cast their blame onto the Horde! I cannot know whether there is any truth to their claim, but their incursion onto OUR land must not stand. Kill first, investigate later!
