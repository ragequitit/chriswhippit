In Service of Zephras

Quest · Zephras Isle · Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 230

Quest text

The High Order have done our best to avoid direct conflict with the cult, but that is clearly no longer an option. Their hostility has been clearly demonstrated, and now there are rumors of larger-scale cult movements around the island. They are clearly gearing up for something and whatever it is can't be good for the people of Zephras. Report to Valennia Stormfist and inform her that the High Order offers our full support in Valanaar's efforts against the Al'Aketh, should the need arise.
