Quest text

Objective: Bring the Broken Samophlange to Wenikee Boltbucket.

I think I might have broken that Samophlange. That's not good, because I already told some colleagues in Undermine about it and they were very intrigued. We must find a way to fix it! Take the broken samophlange to Wenikee Boltbucket, just south of Mor'shan Rampart, the horde outpost between the Barrens and Ashenvale. She is very clever and inquisitive. If you show her the samophlange I bet she'd jump at the chance to fix it.
