Tomb Weed

Quest · Tirisfal Glades · Horde
Level ~9Required –XP 875

Quest text

If you're heading east, towards the Balnir Farmstead, I have more you might do for me. Good help is ever so hard to find these days, as your run-in with Gordo illuminates ever so clearly. As I acquire doom weed I perform some minor alchemical alterations and replant them on that farmstead to cultivate my ultimate creation. I call it tomb weed, and it shall be the herald of my glorious destiny. Do keep an eye on the Scourge in the area while you're there. They seem... drawn... to the weed. Curious.
