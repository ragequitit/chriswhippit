Longwalker Malah

Quest · Mulgore · Horde

Quest text

The Longwalkers are our people's most trusted scouts. Though their loyalty lies with the High Chieftain alone, young Baine at times seeks their aid. This is one such time. Longwalker Malah was dispatched near our borders to the east and did not return last night after three days. When this occurs, it is our duty to confirm their safety. I must ensure the village remains safe. Prove yourself, and seek Malah out. You will know her by her headband and feathered staff, emblematic of the Longwalkers.
