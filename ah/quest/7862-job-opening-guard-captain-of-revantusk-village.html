Quest text

Objective: You have been tasked with the decimation of 20 Vilebranch Berserkers, 20 Vilebranch Shadow Hunters, 20 Vilebranch Blood Drinkers, and 20 Vilebranch Soul Eaters.$B$BShould you complete this task, return to Primal Torntusk at Revantusk Village in the Hinterlands.

With the loss of Guard Captain Malkhor, the position of Guard Captain of Revantusk is once again open. If you are interested in becoming Captain of the Guard for Revantusk, complete the following tasks and submit your application to Primal Torntusk: Exterminate Vilebranch Berserkers. Exterminate Vilebranch Shadow Hunters. Exterminate Vilebranch Blood Drinkers. Exterminate Vilebranch Soul Eaters.
