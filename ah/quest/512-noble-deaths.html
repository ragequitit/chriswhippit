Objective: Bring 7 Alterac Signet Rings to Magistrate Maleb in Southshore.

I fear the leadership behind the Syndicate is bent on Southshore's ruin. We must strike hard at these leaders before their plans are realized. Enter the ruins of Strahnbrad, in the Alterac Mountains to the north. We think the Syndicate is using the area as a headquarters. We know this gang of cutthroats is run by former Alterac nobles.
