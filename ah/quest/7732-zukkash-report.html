Objective: Deliver the Camp Mojache Zukk'ash Report to Zilzibin Drumlore. He resides in the Drag of Orgrimmar.

You've done doubly well in addressing the threat that the Zukk'ash insects pose to Camp Mojache. The threat these things pose is far from over, and I'd like to ask you for one last task on behalf of the Horde. This is a document that details all the information we have learned about these Zukk'ash insects. I want you to deliver it to Zilzibin Drumlore in the Drag of Orgrimmar. He's an expert on alien creatures like these; I hope he can do something with the lore we've obtained.
