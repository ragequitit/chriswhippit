Quest text

Objective: Speak with Brother Paxton in the Northshire Abbey.

The Stresses of Iron? Yes, I know the book. Quite a good one, written by the Dwarven miner Margulf Blaggon a few decades ago. He spent most his time deep in the mountains of Khaz Modan, digging ore. Smart fellow! But where was I? Oh yes, the Stresses of Iron! I'm afraid the book was moved to the Northshire Abbey. The caretaker of the Abbey's library, Brother Paxton, wanted to make a copy of the book for his own shelves. But that was months ago. I wonder why it's taking him so long...
