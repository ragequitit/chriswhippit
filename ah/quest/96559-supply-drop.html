Quest text

Every Gnomish test pilot knows that one must be prepared for the worst-case scenario when taking to our great skies! That's why we never leave the hangar without our pilot survival kits. They have a self-activating beacon attached that will radio our position home as soon as you open it. Not to mention being stuffed full of other useful supplies. If you can find that pack wherever it fell, by all means open it immediately and help yourself to the contents. You'll still be doing me a favor!
