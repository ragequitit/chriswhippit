I have a confession to make. Lady Amberhall did send me to collect those samples, but in truth I volunteered. There's nothing to learn. They're beasts, plain and simple, and should be put down like beasts. We must slay their master at the bottom of this cave and bring an end to this. Surely my mistress will understand.
