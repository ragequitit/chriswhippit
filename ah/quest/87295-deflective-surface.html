Deflective Surface

Quest · Blasted Lands · Horde & Alliance
Level ~55Required –XP 5100Money 1g 2s

Quest text

You may have seen the Redstone basilisks wandering the wastes outside while you were on your way to Nethergarde Keep. They're tough creatures, well adapted to survival in the Blasted Lands. One of their key qualities is their sturdy scales, which reflect heat, protect against sand and wind, and even deflect weapon blows! They're a perfect material for making shields, and if you can bring me a bundle, I'll compensate you well. Only bring ones in good condition!
