Quest text

The Al'Aketh have visited great tragedy upon our enclave, and blood must be repaid with blood. I have an offer for you, stranger. Should you find conflict with these accursed cultists, bring me the ears of those you kill. I will reward you for each that you return with in both coin and, in time, access to the Nightclaw's secrets should you prove yourself to be a true friend.
