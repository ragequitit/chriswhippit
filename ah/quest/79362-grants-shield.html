Grant's Shield

Quest · Duskwood · Alliance
Level ~28Required –XP 2000

Quest text

A shield left behind by the specter of Grant Valor, father of the Valor family and hero of Raven Hill. With Grant's spirit still battling the undead, perhaps his unfinished business was to slay the scourge that injured him in the Raven Hill Cemetery. It is likely the one his mace is still imbedded in.
