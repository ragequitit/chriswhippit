Objective: Bring the Damp Note to Je'neu Sancrea in Ashenvale.

Some poor handwriting is scrawled on the crumpled up note: "Yes, tell all the priestesses to bring the crystals to me. The water elemental spirits are strong within them and they will aid our aquamancers greatly in their own summonings. By controlling the power within the crystals, we will appease Aku'Mai. One day we will have enough power to bring Aku'Mai back to us and the world will return to its greatest era! An era of rule by the Old Gods!"
