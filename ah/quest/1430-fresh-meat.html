Quest text

Objective: Bring 10 Monstrous Crawler Legs to Dar in the Swamp of Sorrows.

There's a high demand for the succulent taste of crawler meat among my customers; especially the meat from the monstrous crawlers. If you plan on heading to the coast, then I could easily make it worth your while to bring me back some of their legs to have transported back to Orgrimmar. The coast is to the east from here, and you should have no problem finding the creatures scurrying along the beaches and under the sea.
