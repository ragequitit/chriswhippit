Quest text

My son, Migi, always takes off to play with his friends all day. I tell him to pick up a lunch at Borstan's, but I know he almost never does, because he comes home starving. These kids think their games are more important than their own bodies! Please, stop at Borstan's Firepit and tell him you're there to deliver Migi's lunch. You can almost certainly find Migi behind Grommash Hold with the rest of his friends. Duties keep me here during the day and I can't watch over his every meal.
