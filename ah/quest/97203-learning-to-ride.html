Quest text

Greetings, {class}. It would seem you are now seasoned enough as an adventurer for me to teach you how to ride a skeletal steed, for a fee. Zachariah will provide you with a free mount once you have trained with me, and after you will be able to purchase any additional mounts you desire. There are many creatures out in the world, and each requires their own riding skill, but you will have to find trainers for each. Speak to me when you have enough coin, and I will teach you about skeletal horses.
