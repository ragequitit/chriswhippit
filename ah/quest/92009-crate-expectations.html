Quest text

There's something you should know about this order. There's a few inverted mana cells padded as well as I could at the bottom of the box. The cells have a tendency to discharge when they get bumped, and when not stabilized in time. It takes about two hours for the contents to fizzle. Kaylaena will know what to do when she receives them, but I suggest going slow if you're going to ride saddled up Mount Hyjal. If you need a refill, come back and I'll top off the canisters. Be careful, you know?
