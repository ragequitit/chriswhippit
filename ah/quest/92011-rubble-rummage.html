Rubble Rummage

Quest · Mount Hyjal · Horde & Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 6200

Quest text

I'm going to need some time to sort out which plans to build first. Something I've been meaning to do is hike up to the old Horde base and see what kind of treasure can be made out of whatever trash is laying around there. If you are heading up there, and have the space to carry it, keep your eyes peeled for scrap metal, wire, or chunks of bat... droppings! Look, it has its uses okay! You can find the remains of the horde encampment far up the mountain, even beyond the Elderwild settlement.
