Quest text

If the forest is as thick with these insects as it sounds, simply killing the workers won't be enough. Go deeper into the forest and find the queen. She'll be close to the hive and surrounded by bigger and more aggressive soldiers, so be careful. Put her down and return here when it's done. That'll be one less problem to think about around here.
