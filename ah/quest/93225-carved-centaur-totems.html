Carved Centaur Totems

Quest · Desolace · Horde
Level ~35Required –XP 2350

Quest text

I have noticed that centaur from different clans sometimes gather away from settlements and engage in trade. I have observed this across numerous nights and seemingly with every clan; most importantly I have seen the currency they use: carved totems. I believe we can turn the tides in our favor with either the Gelkis or the Magram if we deliver a heavy bundle of totems right to them. I have already begun carving some, but I doubt they will be easily fooled without a few authentic ones.
