Terrordale

Quest · Eastern Plaguelands · Horde & Alliance
Level ~60Required –

Quest text

It took me many years to recover from my guilt once my mind was set free from the Lich King. Once liberated by Sylvanas, I rejected the Dark Lady's offer and walked into the plaguelands alone. I wandered for months aimlessly. I'm not sure why I did it but eventually I found my way back to my family's home and what I found there was... unexpected. Travel to the haunted village now known as Terrordale, find the northernmost house and meet me there. You'll learn the rest.
