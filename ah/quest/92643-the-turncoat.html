Quest text

There is a turncoat amongst the Al'Aketh. They claim to know the cult's true intentions for the island and want to come in, but will only do so with assurances of protection. This defector is hiding at a secluded residence just northwest of Valanaar inside the highlands. I need someone who can discreetly go there, find the traitor, and escort him back to us. This might be a trap, but if this information is as important as this traitor claims, it's worth some risk.
