Quest text

Inside the wardrobe, a ledger records trades of hay, lumber, draft animals, and land. Other notes--personal and unfinished--are tucked between its pages. The figures tell their own story: the Sawyer family was skilled at turning timber into weapons, tools, furniture, and wagons alike. Thumbing through the pages reveals other kin of the family. Abigail Sawyer is listed among the relatives. She would be very interested in this.
