Kindling, Kinda

Quest · Mount Hyjal · Horde & Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 4350Money 1g 25s 42c

Quest text

While you're traveling around the mountain, I can't resist asking for another small favor... Did you see all those fel shrubberies and plants littering the hills down below? It turns out they burn rather well, which is both good, and bad. Handy for feeding into a small furnace, but also a risk when it twists together and binds up gears...or bootstraps! Thin their numbers on your way down, and I'll pick up the weeds myself once they've dried out.
