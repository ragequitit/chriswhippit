Violence Into Verdure

Quest · Mount Hyjal · Horde & Alliance

Quest text

The old hulks of the war are decaying and rusting, but we can speed the process of reclamation. Take this bag of the seeds that you collected before. I have given them a small helping of magic to speed their growth. Place them near the old war machinery and let them grow over and cover them. The memories of the war will fade into the past and nature will reclaim its space.
