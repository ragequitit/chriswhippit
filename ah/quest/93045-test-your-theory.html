Quest text

I have placed the skull you provided alongside several bones I found while awaiting your return. Immediately I saw a sickly light spread from the skull and claim those old bones as its own. I see now why the call of necromancy is so hard to resist. Do you remember this crystal? It is draenethyst. I know little of its potential, but I believe it can channel Theradras' power. With it, perhaps you can dispel the curse. The ritual site can be found by the pond just east of the village.
