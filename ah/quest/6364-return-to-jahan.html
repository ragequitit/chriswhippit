Quest text

Objective: Buy a wind rider to the Crossroads from the wind rider master Tal, then bring Ahanu's Leather Goods to Jahan Hawkwing.

In order to fly to a town, you must have already been there and spoken with the wind rider master of that town. You have been to the Crossroads and spoken to their wind rider master Devrak, so for a small fee you can take a wind rider to him from here. Speak with me again when you are ready. Once at the Crossroads, you can then deliver the leather goods to Jahan Hawkwing.
