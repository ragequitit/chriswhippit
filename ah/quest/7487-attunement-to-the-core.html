Quest text

Rifts stir, tear, and collapse all around us, {race}. Not two paces from where I stand is a tear leading through the depths of Blackrock Mountain, into the maw of the Firelord. Surprised? Pity... Venture into Blackrock Depths and retrieve a core fragment. Return it to me and I shall attune your essence with the portal.
