There is a servant of the Lich King that makes his home deep in the quilboar lair known as Razorfen Downs. It is known to us that he makes use of a scrying orb known as "The Coldbringer's Eye" to direct the movements of his lesser minions elsewhere. Go into Razorfen Downs and retrieve the Coldbringer's Eye on our behalf, {class}. The Kirin Tor would pay handsomely to obtain this artifact so that we may study it and understand its inner workings.
