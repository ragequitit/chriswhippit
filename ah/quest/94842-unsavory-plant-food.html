Quest text

We make our own fertilizer out here. It's a special formula of mine that reeks like hell, but grows trees like they're weeds. It's a 1:1:2 ratio of crawfish shells, manure, and murloc heads. Tasty, right? The manure's easy enough. We've got the ranch to the south for that. The heads and shells? Those you're going to have to go wading in the river for. If you feel like lending a hand, we could sure use your help getting some fertilizer ingredients.
