The Broken Construct

Quest · Zephras Isle · Horde
Level ?Required –XP 600

Quest text

Historically, the Windshapers have seen to the tending of the magical instruments and devices that were gifted to us by the spirits of the wind. This includes the myriad of magical constructs around the island. Since the spirits left us, only a scant few of these are still functional. One of the few remaining working constructs in Valanaar has become damaged. One of our artisans was dispatched to help, but he may be in need of aid. Travel to the west end of the city and offer any assistance you can.
