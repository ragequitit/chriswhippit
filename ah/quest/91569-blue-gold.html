Listen kid, everyone out here's scramblin' for the same thing. Belgrom, the elves, the naga, they all wanna piece 'a that ancient highborne magic. Me? Couldn't care less, but I know a seller's market when I see one. I've seen blood elf arcanists carrying special wands. They're used to extract magical energy from artifacts the elves find.
