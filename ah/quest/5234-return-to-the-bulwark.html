Objective: Bring the Filled Writhing Haunt Bottle to Shadow Priestess Vandis at The Bulwark, Western Plaguelands.

You delicately top off the empty bottle; a sample of the foul substance that churns under the platform of the cauldron now oozes back and forth inside the fragile glass container. With the poison in hand, it is time to return to Shadow Priestess Vandis at the Bulwark to complete the mission.
