Eyes of the Sentinels

Quest · Darnassus · Alliance
Level ~53Required –XP 650

Quest text

General Feathermoon ensures all sentinels have time to pray to Elune. A proper huntress wields not blade alone, but gifts of the goddess to overcome our enemies. You may help with my daily duties. Place sentinels around the city--start with the tunnels leading beneath Cenarion Hold to the north, the bank at the center of town, the inn in the Craftsman's Terrace to the northeast, and the city gates. The guards can assist you. Something is amiss in Teldrassil--we must remain vigilant.
