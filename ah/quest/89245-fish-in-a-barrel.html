Quest text

I served in Hearthglen for a time, and I cannot tell you how utterly terrifying it was relying on deliveries from across the Plaguelands to keep us fed. New Avalon having a stable supply chain by sea has been a blessing, at least until very recently. In yet another case of "Carrie is trying to feed an army with a kitchen stocked for a family", I am in need of fish. Check the dock down south to see if my order has arrived.
