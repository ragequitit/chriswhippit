Song of the Strider

Quest · Riverglades · Horde
Level ?Required –XP 3000

Quest text

These markings are from a lion. By the look of them, this one is especially large--the leader of the nearby pride. I would gladly hunt the beast myself, but you have first claim. Instead, I will aid you. I can make a whistle that mimics the call of a wounded tallstrider. You can use it to lure the lion out. I'll just need one of their beaks to give it the right sound. There are tallstriders all over the plains. Find me a beak and the hunt can begin!
