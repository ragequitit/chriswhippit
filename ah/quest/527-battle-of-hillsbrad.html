Objective: Kill 6 Hillsbrad Farmhands, 6 Hillsbrad Farmers, Farmer Ray and Farmer Getz and report back to Darthalia in Tarren Mill.

Stand at attention while addressing me, {class}. I am under direct command of Varimathras. We are to quell the human infestation until our apothecaries can develop the new plague.
