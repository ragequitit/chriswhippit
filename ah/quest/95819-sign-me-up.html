Quest text

Greetings, accomplished craftsperson! Completion of this form will grant you non-exclusive, revocable, and legally limited permission to display advertisement signs for your tradeskill(s) all across Azeroth! Once completed, please bring this to your local Durotar Supply & Logistics Contract Coordinator to begin processing. Happy crafting!
