Quest text

I've known these hills since I was a hatchling. If there is one thing I know, it's that pinecones can solve a lot of problems. Probably not what you expected, eh? <The crow winks at you, conspiratorially.> Go fetch me some; it's part of a little scheme I'm hatching. I promise you it will be worth the effort. You should be able to find them under any healthy or living tree nearby. Stay away from those corrupted ones though, those pinecones aren't any good!
