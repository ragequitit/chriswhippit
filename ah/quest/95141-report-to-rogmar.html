Report to Rog'mar

Quest · Stranglethorn Vale · Horde
Level ~41Required –XP 290

Quest text

Far to the northeast beyond Stonard lies an embattled land along the eastern coast known as the Riverglades. Many have sought to lay claim to these lands, but few have succeeded. There is one, however, whose experience battling our enemies proved enough to settle a small outpost known as Rog'mar. Through countless attacks, it still remains. As ruthless as Krom'rosh may be, his will has served our allies greatly. If it's battle you seek, head northeast to seek out this old friend of ours.
