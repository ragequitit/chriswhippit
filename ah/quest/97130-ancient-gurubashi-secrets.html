Quest text

Lan'dalock and I have been discussing a recent development in the south seas involving an ancient Gurubashi ruin that has suddenly appeared off the coast of Stranglethorn Vale. Such a site has significant archeological value. We'd be highly interested in obtaining one of the idols that certain witch doctors amongst the ancient Gurubashi would use as a ritual focus for their blood magic. Enter this "Drowned City" and recover one of these idols for us.
