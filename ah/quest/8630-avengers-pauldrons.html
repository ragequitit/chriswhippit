Quest text

Objective: Bring the Qiraji Bindings of Dominance, 2 Idols of Life, 5 Crystal Scarabs and 5 Gold Scarabs to Andorgos in Ahn'Qiraj. You must also attain Neutral reputation with the Brood of Nozdormu to complete this quest.

It is said among us that the ornate shoulder armor worn by the mortal races started as an attempt to emulate the wings of a dragon. Bring me the bindings worn by the highest Qiraji leaders and I shall shape them into a set of pauldrons more dreadful than even the wings of Nefarian himself!
