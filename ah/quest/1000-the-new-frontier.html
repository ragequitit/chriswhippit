Objective: Speak with Arch Druid Hamuul Runetotem on the Elder Rise of Thunder Bluff about the Cenarion Circle's call to explore the frontiers of Kalimdor.

Attention, heroes! The Cenarion Circle seeks able bodied individuals to assist them in exploring the vast new frontiers of Kalimdor! A forbidden wasteland of unimaginable dangers lies far to the west of Tanaris and Gadgetzan, and the Circle needs willing and honorable proxies to act in their stead. All interested individuals should seek audience with the Cenarion Circle. Speak with the Arch Druid Hamuul Runetotem on the Elder's Rise of Thunder Bluff for more information!
