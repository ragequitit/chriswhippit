Safety Checks

Quest · Badlands · Alliance
Level ~40Required –XP 3150Money 25s

Quest text

Hullo there, you look like the helpful sort. The name's Dorgaron, and I'm with the Explorers' League. We've not had the best of luck with diggin' in the Badlands, but I know darn well there's artifacts to find and collect! Before I can convince the boss to let us send another crew out here, I need to prove that the dig sites are safe AND haven't been picked clean. If you find yourself down south, gather a few reasonably intact artifacts and some leftover equipment that hasn't been smashed yet.
