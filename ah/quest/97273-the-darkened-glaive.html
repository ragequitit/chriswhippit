The Darkened Glaive

Quest · The Barrow Deeps · Horde & Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 180

Quest text

Return to the depths of the Barrow Deeps. Hunt the demon responsible for the corruption of Quel'Shalla. You must not recoil from the demon's strength, but rather meet it head on. Lean into it and allow the glaive to drink in its power... its very essence. The blade hungers for this and it will let you know when the time is right. Once Quel'Shalla's thirst is slaked, bring its power to bear to unmake the demon forever.
