Quest text

Had to be sure we could trust you. These kind of friends are hard to come by. There's a malignancy growing, a sinister faction within Azeroth. Both the Horde and Alliance are unwittingly working towards their own ends. Slowing their advances and forming information networks are all we have resources for. Take this, raise your spirits, and harken back to better days. There will be a time soon where we need our bolstered numbers for a show of force.
