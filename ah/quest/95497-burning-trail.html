Burning Trail

Quest · Burning Steppes · Alliance
Level ~54Required –XP 725Money 2g 43s

Quest text

We've heard some reports that one of the zeppelins was using the smoky mountains around here as a shipping guide, and crashed into the cliffs. The Undermarket is known for using those hills in the north of burning steppes for their smuggling and bootlegging operations. Go to those hills and investigate for any signs of the lost zeppelin cargo, we may be able to recover key supplies important to the alliance.
