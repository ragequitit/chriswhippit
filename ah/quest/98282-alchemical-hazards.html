Alchemical Hazards

Quest · Wetlands · Alliance
Level ~26Required –XP 1750

Quest text

Certain alchemical recipes require ingredients sourced from, well, very dangerous places. And those are often just the base herbs! The other ingredients are even worse! I need to make more antivenom before I can leave again. I swear everything in this marsh is venomous. It's like it runs through the land itself! Can you help me? I'll need a venom gland from the spiders in Thelgen Rock to the far southeast. But it has to be unruptured, or too much venom will spill out before you can get here. Sorry!
