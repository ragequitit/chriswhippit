Objective: Bring 15 Deepmoss Eggs to Mebok Mizzyrix in Ratchet.

Spider egg omelettes are a new fad in Booty Bay! The problem is... Booty Bay's got no eggs. I smell an opportunity! In Windshear Crag and Sishir Canyon, in the Stonetalon Mountains to the northwest, lives the deepmoss spider. Bring me its eggs and I'll pay a bundle! In Windshear Crag, the eggs will be clustered under what trees remain. In Sishir Canyon... well, if you have a weak stomach then maybe you should just collect the eggs at Windshear Crag...
