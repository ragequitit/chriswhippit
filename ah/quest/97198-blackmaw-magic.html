Quest text

The Blackmaw furbolgs in the mountains overlooking Azshara have been corrupted by dark and sinister magic. We do not know the full extent of the corruption and what impact it may have upon the elements in the area, but we hope to understand it better. Travel to the stronghold known as Blackmaw Hold and recover one of the Blackmaw Ritual Totems for us. Only the most powerful of the Blackmaw's shamans will be carrying one of these, so bring allies and prepare for a difficult battle.
