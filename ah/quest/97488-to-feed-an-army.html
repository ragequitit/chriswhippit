To Feed an Army

Quest · Searing Gorge · Alliance
Level ~50Required –XP 4400Money 83s 50c

Quest text

I'm realizing quick that my stash of food is barely enough to keep the brotherhood fed. How am I supposed to keep up once our own start to show up in greater numbers? I heard the orc around the hill sending Horde toward the worgs on the far southeast side of the gorge. I think you should kill a few and bring back their meat, but you can focus most of your efforts on the lava spiders to the west to hopefully minimize some competition with the enemy. Don't worry, I know how to cook a delicious spider.
