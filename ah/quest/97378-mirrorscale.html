Quest text

<name>, you have proven you are a strong and dutiful hunter and I am impressed by your bravery. It is only because of this I offer you this hunt. A wind serpent hunts along a road off the coast of Feralas and has been ambushing travelers, and leaving their corpses strewn about the road. It is clear she does not hunt out of necessity. This is a creature that no longer shares the Earthmother's blessing. The windserpent Mirrorscale makes her lair at the bottom of the waterfall along the coast of Feralas.
