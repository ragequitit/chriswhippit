The Darkened Glaive

Quest · Mount Hyjal · Horde & Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 180

Quest text

I could smell the stink of demonic corruption on this glaive from the start. The demon that influenced it must be powerful, indeed. Particularly if the Shen'dralar--of all elves--want nothing to do with it. Pouring the level of corruption that would be needed to taint a deeply magical artifact such as one of the storied "Chromatic Blades" would leave a connection. If you have the will, you can exploit that connection. I will explain what is needed.
