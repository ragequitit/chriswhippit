Quest text

Objective: Enter Naxxramas and uncover the Fate of Ramaladni.

Ramaladni was a warrior of the Brotherhood - a pupil of Highlord Mograine before the Dawn even existed! When news of Mograine's death reached him, hatred and anger took over. Understand this, <name>, when rage and retribution take the reins, a man ceases to be a man. The man personifies the emotions that control him instead. And so it would be that Ramaladni, the spirit of retribution, would enter Naxxramas to exact vengeance... and never return. Enter Naxxramas and find what became of Ramaladni.
