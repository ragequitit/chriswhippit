Drum Up Trouble

Quest text

There is a fel chimaera terrorizing beasts and travelers along hills. It is known to the Cenarion Circle as Ghansurok. I'll handle the aim and firing of the glaive thrower. Go to that drum by the cliff side. Let's see this blight driven from the mountain for good. I'll only have a chance of shooting it down if you can draw its attention. Once you have the beast by the ridge, find some place to hide! Yes, I promise I have a full bag of maple seeds!
