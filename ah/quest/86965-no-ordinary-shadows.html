No Ordinary Shadows

Quest · Deadwind Pass · Horde & Alliance
Level ?Required –

Quest text

A sudden and oppressive darkness begins to fall over you as you kneel down. You notice that all nearby light seems to darken and fade as the darkness seems to reach out to you. These are no natural shadows. A quick glance over to the entrance to the crypt nearby tells you without doubt that this malign darkness is coming from the crypt itself, with inky black tendrils seeking more light to snuff out. You shudder as hairs on the back of your neck stand up.
