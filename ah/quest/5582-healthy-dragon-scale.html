Objective: Bring the Healthy Dragon Scale to Betina Bigglezink at the Light's Hope Chapel in Eastern Plaguelands.

This is a scale shed from a plagued dragon hatchling. But unlike the other pocked, oozing scales on the infant beast, this rare scale is healthy and unmarred. Betina Bigglezink will want a sample of this to study.
