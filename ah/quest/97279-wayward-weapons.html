Wayward Weapons

Quest · Valley of Trials · Horde
Level ~4Required –XP 170

Quest text

With an influx of new recruits like yourself, it's been hard to keep our weapons supplied. Our weaponsmith, Kzan, is running low on training weapons and I'd like to see what kinds of skills you possess to get an idea of what I have to work with. Luckily for you, I have a solution to both. While you're out there cutting your teeth on boars, salvage six abandoned training weapons. Our failed recruits left them laying on the ground. Once you do, return them to our weaponsmith Kzan behind me in the Den.
