Kits and Cats

Quest · Mount Hyjal · Horde & Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 4500

Quest text

Oh, a tasty morsel... Fear not, I jest. Though I could snap your spine like a dry branch. I am a demigod of the wilds, after all. If you would gain my favor and avoid my appetite, perhaps I could burden you with a task? My litter of young have wandered off to entertain themselves. Little on this mountain could threaten them directly, but there are dark things afoot near the summit, and I'd leave nothing to chance. Would you check on them? In return for my not eating you, of course.
