A Thorny Solution

Quest · Mount Hyjal · Horde & Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 180Money 23s

Quest text

We are constantly under threat here, both by the Blackthorne Pact and by the hostile creatures of Hyjal. But we can use the thorns to our advantage! With a little help from the power of nature, we can grow new thorn bushes to help protect our enclosure. Plant thorn seeds near the road to help create barricades by the inn, the storage tower, and the grouping of trees near the road.
