Bounty: Gnarlpine Furbolg

Quest · Teldrassil · Alliance
Level ~9Required –XP 700Money 2s 25c

Quest text

By strict orders from the Sentinels, I am commissioning a bounty on the Gnarlpine. They are no longer friends of the forest. Their corruption has left them mindless threats to our people and the creatures of the glade. As decreed, you shall be rewarded for removing the furbolg menaces from Teldrassil. Bring to me their fangs as proof of your deeds. You will find furbolg concentrated in Ban'ethil Hollow, west of Dolanaar and south of the road, and in Starbreeze Village to the east.
