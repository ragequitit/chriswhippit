Dawn in the Mountains

Quest · Dun Morogh · Alliance
Level ~9Required –XP 70

Quest text

There's a priest out in the mountains that belongs to an organization called the Argent Dawn. They're a holy group that is dedicated to finding and destroying the unholy and unnatural. Father Gavin though, he's mostly the type that seeks to help and heal everyone rather than grab a weapon. He's fixed up an old building to serve as a rest spot for travelers and called it Misty Pine Refuge. I've got a shipment of holy candles that I need to get him. Could you bring them to him if you're going that way?
