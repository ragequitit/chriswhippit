Buying Friendship: Magram

Quest · Desolace · Horde & Alliance
Level ~35Required –XP 390

Quest text

Siding with the Magram would be my choice. There is no question that they are the dominant clan in the present day, regardless of their previous struggles. I have heard reports that they are brutally hostile against the Gelkis. I sympathize with both warring clans and especially the Gelkis, but this is their war; not ours. We ought to side with the faction that poses the least risk to our cause. I believe that to be the Magram. Bring this to their quartermaster in Magram Village to the southeast.
