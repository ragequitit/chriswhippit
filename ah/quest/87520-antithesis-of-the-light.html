Antithesis of the Light

Quest · Eastern Plaguelands · Horde & Alliance
Level ~60Required –XP 725Money 1g 80s

Quest text

The smooth black artifact before you pulsates ominously. It's as if this object seems to draw in and consume all nearby light. Your intuition tells you that this object is dangerous, and that touching it with your bare hands would be ill advised. You wrap the object in a cloth and stash it in your pack. Lord Maxwell Tyrosus at Light's Hope Chapel may be interested in this.
