Quest text

This skeleton looks different than the others you've seen in these catacombs. Although unmoving, there seems to be a very faint spark of something burning inside its hollow eyes. You hear a faint buzzing sound in your head. There are no words, but it seems to be somehow... pleading for something. An image forms in your mind of a small chest containing a faintly glowing urn. You are being shown the phylactery of a lich.
