Quest text

Just north of here lies the ruins of battles past. A dwindling scourge presence remains. Though the mindless undead are a threat themselves, the watchers maintain a vigilant eye on those entering the area, making removal of the scourge threat even more difficult. Remove the threat by taking from these watchers that which they value most.
