Business in Auberdine

Quest · Darkshore · Alliance
Level ~16Required –XP 625

Quest text

Pleasure to make yer acquaintance! Name's Borgus, of the one and only Steelhand family. You can always count on a Steelhand fer a fair deal! A few weeks back me brother Gorbold joined up with a merchant stall in Auberdine. Plenty of coin to be made supplyin' ships there, let me tell you! Lucky sod. I haven't heard from him since--mind checking on him if yer heading that way? There's a new ship in the harbor that will take ye straight there. Head to the southern-most dock; can't miss it!
