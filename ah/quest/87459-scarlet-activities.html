Quest text

While we play nice with the Scarlet Crusaders here, they continue to behave suspiciously elsewhere. Some of my contacts in Tirisfal tell me of a daring Scarlet Crusade incursion into the heart of the Undercity itself, and we've received reports from our scouts of large caravans moving supplies through the plaguelands. A band of elite scarlet forces has been spotted near the Crusader Outpost in Tirisfal Glades, between the Balnir Farmstead and Venomweb Vale. If you would, go and see what you can find there.
