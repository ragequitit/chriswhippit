Objective: Return the Venture Co. Documents to Morin Cloudstalker near Bloodhoof Village.

Prying open the lid reveals a tightly packed assortment of ore and minerals, more evidence of the Venture Co.'s mining operation in Mulgore. On the top of the ore and minerals you remove a tightly bound sheaf of papers. Up in the foothills to the east, you can barely hear the sounds of work, and see smoke rising from a large fire.
