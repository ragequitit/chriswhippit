The Hydra's Head

Quest text

It is time to cut off the heads of this hydra, if you catch my meaning. There are three leaders who need to be killed in order to slow the operations here. Conveniently, they also need to be slain to remove the wards on my cage. Tyaeha can be found in the House of Diseases by the creepy tree. Dazka is in the Alchemist ward with all the potions, and that fool Frezzlie is undoubtedly next to the slime pits along the northern ridge of the encampment. Go slay them and bring me their bones!
