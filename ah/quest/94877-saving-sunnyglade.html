Saving Sunnyglade

Quest text

When you look around you, what do you see? A broken home and a tired people. Stormwind has left us to fend for ourselves, and we have less to do that with every day. Many within these walls are refugees who barely escaped tragedy without so much as even their own families. Farholde is no Stormwind, stranger. We lost a settlement in the northwest to the Twilight's Hammer, but we will not let them keep it. If you wish to fight, go to Sunnyglade and purge those savages from our lands.
