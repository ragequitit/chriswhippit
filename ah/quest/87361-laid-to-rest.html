Quest text

Plucked from a Shadow of Doom, the heart faintly pulses and vibrates. You move to put it in your bag, but are struck with an overwhelming sense of anger, fear, dread, and then... sadness. As these feeling subside, you feel as though you've just lived another life. Perhaps someone at Light's Hope Chapel can shed some light on what you just felt.
