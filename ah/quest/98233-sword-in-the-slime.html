Sword in the Slime

Quest · Dustwallow Marsh · Alliance
Level ~37Required –XP 3450Money 55s

Quest text

I was having a drink with my buddies over at the tavern a few nights back and they dared me to try and take on one of those nasty oozes alone. Seeing as I don't have a sword, you can probably guess how well that went. When I went to attack it my sword got stuck and things didn't go as planned. I need to get it back before my sergeant realizes I lost it! It should be stuck in an ooze out there somewhere, west of here across the bay. Please, if you're headed out that way, can you look for it?
