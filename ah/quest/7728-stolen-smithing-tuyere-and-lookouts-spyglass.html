Objective: Find and return the Smithing Tuyere and Lookout's Spyglass to Taskmaster Scrange in the Searing Gorge.$B$BThe only information you have about these items is the following: They were definitely stolen by Dark Iron dwarves. The Smithing Tuyere is a blacksmithing tool used by blacksmiths and the Lookout's Spyglass is an invaluable monitoring tool to lookouts.

The following items have been stolen: *One Dark Iron Tuyere. *One Lookout's Spyglass. A reward is being offered to individuals who find and return these pilfered items. The Thorium Brotherhood is certain that these items were stolen by unscrupulous and jealous Dark Iron dwarves that inhabit the Searing Gorge. The spyglass in question was an excellent tool for the lookouts and the tuyere was an irreplaceable smithing tool. Should you find the missing items, return them to Scrange.
