Keeping the Light On

Quest · Riverglades · Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 2150Money 30s

Quest text

While we no longer hold service at the church, I feel it important to keep the candles lit on the graves of our fallen in the church's crypt. To me, keeping the candle's flame alive is a symbol that they are not forgotten and their sacrifices were not in vain. If you would, bring me candles so that I might continue this ritual. It would mean a great deal to me. If you're not sure where to find them, speak with Marla in the house just outside of the church. She's a friend and may have some to spare.
