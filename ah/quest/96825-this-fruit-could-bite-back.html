This Fruit Could Bite Back

Quest · Durotar · Horde
Level ~9Required –XP 550

Quest text

Our hunters and fishers bring back plenty of savory food for our troops - but it's rare that they get a sweet treat. I've seen a cactus that produces a prickly, tasty fruit. But it only grows in the hills to the west, where the wretched quilboars make their camps. Make your way past the quilboars and bring me some of those fruits. And be careful - the fruits are as spiny as the quilboars!
