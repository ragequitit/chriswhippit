Quest text

<name>, we Darkspear keep much of our traditions alive by oral storytelling. We pass legends such as these to the next generation, teaching lessons and weaving cautionary tales to our younglings. These etchings must be taken to our spiritual leader, Master Gadrin in Sen'jin Village. He is one of the wisest Darkspear and he will wish to hear the truth of this tale. I have prepared an extra copy of this tale for you as well. If you ever lose your copy, I would be happy to create another, for a small fee.
