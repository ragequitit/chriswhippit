A Hunter's Quiver

Quest · Riverglades · Horde
Level ?Required –XP 3150

Quest text

As important as a hunter's weapons is her quiver. A good quiver holds many arrows, while not being so large as to slow the wearer down. It must be durable, and fit snugly onto the body. Around here, there is no better material for a quiver than the pelts of lions. They are already well-suited for the life of a hunter on the plains. The lions gather on a hill to the south. You can see it from here. Please, bring me their pelts.
