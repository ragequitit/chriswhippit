Challenger of the Gods

Quest text

Ve'han's research notes describe his attempts to steal the power of the Wild Gods, following the ambitions of his leader, the Wild King. His successes were few, and hapless. Had he been a more powerful druid, he likely would have quickly devoured the essence of Jereman and gained even more power still. The Guardians of Hyjal at the summit camp will definitely want to know about this.
