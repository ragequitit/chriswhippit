The Orphan

Quest · Blasted Lands · Alliance
Level ~55Required –XP 4100Money 1g 22s 25c

Quest text

We often try to find surviving family for many of our charges, and occasionally we find something hopeful. I've received word that Randal here may have a surviving family member. The man's name is Danneth, and he is a soldier stationed at Nethergarde Keep. I need someone to travel there and find the man. If you can, please convince Danneth to come retrieve his nephew. I don't think the two have ever met, but they are family all the same and Randal belongs with him.
