Gnomish Garbage

Quest · Tanaris · Alliance
Level ~57Required –XP 2900

Quest text

The Matter Transporter required experimentation to get right. Many tests were conducted on all sorts of materials... with various outcomes! Some of the results are no longer with us. Others have become dangerously charged! These subjected objects emit an energy that burns if you stay near for too long. Almost like they've been charged with a year's worth of the desert sun's power. Take this Crate of Radioactive Refuse and empty the contents in the sand a safe distance from town and other trash.
