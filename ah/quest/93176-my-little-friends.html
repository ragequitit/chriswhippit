My Little Friends

Quest · Swamp of Sorrows · Horde & Alliance
Level ~43Required –XP 2750Money 35s

Quest text

Admiring my little menagerie, are you? They are my un-life's work. I've spent countless hours studying them: their anatomy, their diets, even their social rituals. Do you wish to join me in the hobby? Here, take this package. The notes and materials contained within should get you started. If you lose it, you'll have to purchase another one from me for a small fee.
