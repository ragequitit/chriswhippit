Objective: Bring 12 Timberling Sprouts to Denalan at Lake Al'Ameth.

Small timberlings are sprouting around the waters of Lake Al'Ameth. I'm afraid these sprouts are beyond help -- we should try to clear them from the land before they grow large enough to cause trouble. When you're wandering the lake, if you see any timberling sprouts please take them. Help keep our land clean!
