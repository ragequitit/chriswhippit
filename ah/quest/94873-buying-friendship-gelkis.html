Buying Friendship: Gelkis

Quest · Desolace · Horde & Alliance
Level ~35Required –XP 390

Quest text

Either dominant centaur clan is likely to pose a good ally, but I ought to offer a word of caution regarding the Gelkis. I have trustworthy intel that suggests, between the Magram and the Gelkis, the latter are very much on the losing side. This may work to our advantage--desperation may push the isolated clan into opening up to outside help. I have prepared a tote of totems for you to take to their quartermaster, assuming they have one, in Gelkis Village. This may work in our favor.
