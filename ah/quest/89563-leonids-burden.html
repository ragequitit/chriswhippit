Leonid's Burden

Quest · Eastern Plaguelands · Horde & Alliance
Level ~60Required –XP 180Money 23s

Quest text

I could not help but overhear your conversation with Leonid about Inquisitor Caldoran. The scant details Leonid provided to you are the barest sliver of the history they share and the suffering Caldoran has inflicted on him. I was there when Leonid was at his lowest point and I helped him find the strength to pick himself back up. I fear what our continued cooperation with the Scarlet Crusade will do to Leonid, particularly if it brings him closer to that man.
