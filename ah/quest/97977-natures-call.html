Nature's Call

Quest · Shadowglen · Alliance
Level ~4Required –XP 250Money 50c

Quest text

The Gnarlpine furbolg would not turn on their ancient allies so readily. We must learn more of their plight. Search their abandoned homes for totems. If any survived the chaos of their departure, their engravings may tell us more of what happened here. You'll find their camps along the western edge of the glen, though the grell and grellkin may have scattered them beyond.
