Beneath Nordrassil

Quest · Azshara · Alliance
Level ~56Required –XP 550

Quest text

You may have noticed the furbolg and satyr north of here fighting over a burrow. The conflict does not interest me as much as what could be inside. That burrow seems to lead beneath Nordrassil. I don't think I need to tell you how this could be of interest to the Kaldorei. We have our hands full here as it is, but the druids of Darnassus should be informed. Find Mathrengyl Bearwalker in the Cenarion Enclave. He can decide if this is something worth pursuing.
