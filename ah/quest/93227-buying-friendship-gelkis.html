Buying Friendship: Gelkis

Quest · Desolace · Horde & Alliance
Level ~35Required –XP 390

Quest text

At first glance, I would wager that the Gelkis are struggling. Their numbers are far fewer than the Magram, and I rarely hear of them stomping around from our scouts. Nonetheless, they are one of the dominant clans in the Desolace and survive to this day. They must be doing something right. If you want to make peace with the Gelkis, take this tote of totems with you to Gelkis Village far to the southwest. Primitive as they may be, they are likely to have a treasurer of some kind.
