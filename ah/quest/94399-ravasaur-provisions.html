Ravasaur Provisions

Quest · Un'Goro Crater · Horde
Level ~53Required –

Quest text

The Ravasaur in Un'Goro are entirely carnivorous. That means they only eat meat. This place may be chock full of resources, but that just means the jungle can turn into a bloodbath on the rare occasion when prey becomes scarce. All across Un'Goro, you are going to find diemetradons and pterrodaxes. Bring me their meats!
