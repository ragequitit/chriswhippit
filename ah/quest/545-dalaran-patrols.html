Quest text

Objective: Kill 6 Dalaran Summoners and 12 Elemental Slaves, then return to Magus Voidglare in Tarren Mill.

Before it mysteriously vanished, there was an enormous magical barrier around the newly-reconstructed city of Dalaran. I care not what caused such a magical marvel to fail, but I do care that the newly exposed outskirts of their city may hide valuable magical treasures. Vestiges of the ruins on which the new city is built. But the area is patrolled by the wizards and their elemental slaves. So hunt them, and return when their numbers are sufficiently reduced.
