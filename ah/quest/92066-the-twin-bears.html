Quest text

When the Legashi invaded, many precious things were left behind in the commotion. Of particular value to me are two totems. They depict legends of our twin gods, Ursoc and Ursol. I know our immediate survival is of greatest concern, but we must also think of saving our people's history. Those totems are likely still in our former hold. If they are, you would be doing a great kindness by returning them to me.
