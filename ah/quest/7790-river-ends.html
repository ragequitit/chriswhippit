River Ends

Quest · Eastern Plaguelands · Horde & Alliance
Level ~60Required –XP 600

Quest text

A grisly scene, these two elves appear to have died locked in combat to the end. What could have brought such viciousness to their normally reserved demeanor? Their bags and equipment are strewn along the shore. Was it a deal gone bad? A rival's ambush? Search the source of the river for clues to the demise that was washed downstream.
