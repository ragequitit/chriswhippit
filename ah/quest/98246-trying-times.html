Trying Times

Quest · Wetlands · Alliance
Level ~26Required –XP 2350

Quest text

I'll need some eggs if my plan is going tae work. Bassbeak needs a nice an' full belly--I ain't in the business of hoppin' on the back of a surly gryphon. They need to be perfect--no cracks or anything. Take some from the Razormaw raptors--they're known for having yolks the size of a gnome's noggin. You can find 'em in Raptor Ridge to the northeast and Saltspray Glen to the northwest. Let's make it a nice even dozen, just to be safe.
