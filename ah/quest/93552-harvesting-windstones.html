Quest text

Windstones are a critical resource on Zephras Isle as they contain the raw, concentrated energy of the winds themselves. The wind elementals who used to live here on Zephras with us vanished a number of years ago, and since then, the magic that sustains this island is slowly fading. Not without limitations, the windstones are still a reliable energy source, and are incredibly valuable to us. You'll find raw windstones scattered around the grove. Think you can help me harvest them?
