Gettin' Buzzed

Quest · Azshara · Horde
Level ~56Required –XP 4400

Quest text

There are few luxuries out here on the frontier. A barrel of good drink would surely boost the camp's morale. In my younger days, I took a recipe for a delicious brew off the corpse of a dwarf I killed. All I need is some honey. Fortunately, the wasp nests to the west are overflowing with it. Take my pellet gun. This will allow you to knock the nests out of the trees. Be warned, however, it will do little against the wasps themselves.

Rewards

  • Berdun's Trusty Rifle
  • Recipe: Potion of Beast Slaying
