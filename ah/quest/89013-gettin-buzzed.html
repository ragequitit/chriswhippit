Gettin' Buzzed

Quest · Azshara · Alliance
Level ~56Required –XP 4400

Quest text

What's a travel lodge without somethin' to drink? I can brew a fine mead, but I'm gettin' a bit on in years to gather the honey myself. There are wasp nests in the trees north of the Haldaar camp, an' west of Valormok. If ye see hippogryphs, yer in the right place. They're nasty buggers, but the honey is like nothin' ye've ever tasted. I'll lend ye my pellet gun to knock down the hives, ye can't reach 'em otherwise.
