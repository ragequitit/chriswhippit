Reclaiming Ravencrest

Quest · Azshara · Alliance
Level ~56Required –XP 5650Money 1g 67s 83c

Quest text

I fear time may soon forget the Kaldorei house of Ravencrest. A lone, crumbling, monument is the only surviving proof of their presence here. The monument can be found atop the cliffs at the very tip of the southern peninsula. Spitelash enchantresses there have a set of power stones which they use to channel their magic. Take this ward, and nullify them. This will cripple the Spitelash's magic, and give us an upper hand in driving them out. While you're at it, be sure to take out a few naga yourself.
