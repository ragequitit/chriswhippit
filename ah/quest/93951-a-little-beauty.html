A Little Beauty

Quest · Zephras Isle · Horde & Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 480Money 1s 25c

Quest text

The hippogryphs that have taken up residence southwest of the village can be a nuisance, but they also present a rather unique opportunity to obtain their feathers. Many skyborne love to adorn their hair with vibrant plumes and the down feathers of a hippogryph are exceptionally vibrant and, more importantly, silky soft. If you are heading that way, try and gather as many of the fallen feathers as you can from around their nesting grounds. Do be careful, as wild hippogryph are not tolerant of guests.
