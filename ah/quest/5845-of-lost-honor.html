Quest text

Objective: Travel to Northdale, in the northeastern region of the Eastern Plaguelands, and recover the Symbol of Lost Honor. Return to Tirion Fordring upon completion of your objective.

The Order of the Silver Hand was utterly decimated when Uther was slain. The boy held out for as long as he could. Pushed to the war torn hamlet of Northdale, he made his final stand. Were any of the Order left alive, he thought - and did it matter? It was with that thought that Taelan threw down the standard of the Order and renounced all that he had known. His honor left upon the blood soaked ground of Northdale. You must travel to Northdale and recover that symbol of lost honor.
