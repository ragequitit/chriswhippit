Bark Bite

Quest · Mount Hyjal · Horde & Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 575

Quest text

With the ingredients you brought, I can make some simple medicine. This poultice should aid Fraxinus. He is old, you know, and he was badly burned during the wars. His bark hurts and his limbs ache. He is my best friend and I just want him to feel better! Take this poultice and apply it liberally to all of the places where his bark is split and his sap is weeping.
