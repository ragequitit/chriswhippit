Quest · The Barrens · Horde

Quest text

These hides are bound for a tanner who I often trade with. He is something of a hermit and he lives alone on the top of the ridge behind me. Climbing up a steep hillside isn't something I can manage easily right now so I'd be in your debt if you could make this delivery for me. Oh, and about this tanner's... appearance. You need not be alarmed. In spite of his looks, he is no threat. I swear this is true.
