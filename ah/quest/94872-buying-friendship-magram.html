Buying Friendship: Magram

Quest · Desolace · Horde & Alliance
Level ~35Required –XP 390

Quest text

The fierce Magram are undoubtedly winning the war of territories at the moment. While we can all be thankful the clashes haven't erupted too frequently throughout the desert, every day their village just to the east grows a little bigger; their soldiers a little bolder. I worry gravely about the consequences of an unchecked Magram clan, but I cannot deny their potential as an ally. If you wish to earn their trust, offer this sack of totems to their quartermaster, but be careful.
