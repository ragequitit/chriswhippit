Quest text

Objective: Search Dire Maul for Kariel Winthalus. Report back to Sage Korolusk at Camp Mojache with whatever information that you may find.

Several years ago, a lone elven master named Telmius Dreamseeker fled the devastation of his homeland with several ancient elven artifacts in tow. Our search for this lost master lead us from the ruins of Quel'Thalas, through the Burning Steppes, across the great expanses of the sea, to Feralas. We suspect that the elf sought sanctuary in the halls of Eldre'Thalas (what is now known as Dire Maul). You must find him, adventurer. Whether alive or dead, the knowledge he carried must be recovered!
