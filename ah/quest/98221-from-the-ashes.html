From the Ashes

Quest · Wetlands · Alliance
Level ~26Required –XP 2300

Quest text

In the Second War the Dragonmaw built a fleet where we now stand, intending to take Hillsbrad. They nearly succeeded! They were routed by the red dragons, left to mere stragglers deep in the mountains. However, there has been a surge in activity in their encampment northeast of Menethil. Dun Algaz is proof that they intend to march to war once again and retake this land. Find their attack plans, take note of any critical information, and destroy them. We need to know where they will strike next!
