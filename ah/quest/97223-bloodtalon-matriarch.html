Bloodtalon Matriarch

Quest · Durotar · Horde
Level ~9Required –XP 775

Quest text

All of the raptors you see before you are ones I have trained myself. They come from all over Kalimdor, but some of the finest come from the Bloodtalon raptors on the isles off our coast. One raptor amongst them, the Bloodtalon Matriarch, is particularly savage and has killed many from our village and has become too dangerous to leave at our borders. If you can strike her down, return with her eggs so that we can raise the hatchlings as allies instead of enemies.
