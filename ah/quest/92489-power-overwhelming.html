Quest text

The Kirin Tor have long been allies of the Alliance. With the destruction of their city at the hands of the arch demon Archimonde, the once proud city of mages has now become reclusive and distrusting of outsiders. In recent years they have begun reconstruction efforts to bring the city of Dalaran back to its former glory. However, reports show they are struggling to contain an outbreak of wayward arcane energies. We must help them disperse these attacks lest we lose one of our most powerful allies.
