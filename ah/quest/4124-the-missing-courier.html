Objective: Speak with Ginro Hearthkindle in Feathermoon Stronghold.

An associate of mine, Ginro Hearthkindle, is looking for assistance concerning the location of a courier and the parcels he was carrying. It has been a week or so since the courier went missing; our own searches have proved fruitless, and we're at an impasse. Unfortunately, I have had to declare the courier as missing in action. If you have the time and resources to spare, please speak with Ginro. He should be here in the Stronghold - perhaps in his quarters.
