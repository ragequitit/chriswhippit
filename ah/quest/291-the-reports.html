Objective: Deliver Senir's report to Senator Barin Redstone in Ironforge.

Excellent! Take my report to Senator Barin Redstone. He's a sour type, so don't let his less-than-sunny disposition get to you. He's in Ironforge, in the chamber where King Magni holds court. Don't know the way to Ironforge? Take the road out of Kharanos north, cross the bridge, then follow the road east--there are large banners flanking the road--up the mountainside. By the way, if you might avoid mentioning how you assisted me? Can't let them think I'm not working hard out here, you know?
