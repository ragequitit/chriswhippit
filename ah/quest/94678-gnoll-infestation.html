Gnoll Infestation

Quest · Riverglades · Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 2850

Quest text

The gnolls have been a thorn in our side ever since we arrived. While less a threat than the Twilight's Hammer, they are aggressive, persistent and, worst of all, numerous. The hills beyond this post are infested with them, making passage to the north dangerous, and posing a threat to those living outside the walls of Farholde Keep. If we are ever to make these lands safe, removing the gnoll threat is imperative.
