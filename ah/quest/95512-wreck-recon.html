Wreck Recon

Quest · Burning Steppes · Horde
Level ~54Required –XP 725Money 2g 43s

Quest text

We've heard some reports that the zeppelins use the smoky mountains around here as a shipping guide, and one crashed into the cliffs. The Undermarket is known for using those hills in the north of Burning Steppes for their smuggling and bootlegging operations. Go to those hills and investigate for any signs of the lost zeppelin cargo, we may be able to recover items important to the Horde.
