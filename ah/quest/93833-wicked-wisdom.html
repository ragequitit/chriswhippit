Quest text

The owls of Hyjal are more than feathers and flight. Their talons, their plumes, even the bones whisper of the wild. We use these gifts to craft talismans, charms that bind the spirits and shield our kin. But the owls do not give freely. You must take what the mountain offers. Kill oa owls and bring their carcasses to me. Yes, we ask you to return the whole body to us, as we waste nothing provided by such graceful creatures.
