Objective: Kill Infiltrator Hameya. Use his key on the Mound of Dirt behind the Undercroft.

Kill me. Before it's too late. I am Hameya. While traveling to Zul'Mashar, my brethren and I ate some bad meat... and we changed. We are infected. I can feel the lich's words now, urging me to reach Zul'Mashar and infect more of my people. I cannot resist, and so I wrote this scroll before my will left me. I buried a chest in dirt next to a wagon behind this crypt, but only my key will open it. I have the key. If you want my treasure, then find me. Kill me. Before it's too late.
