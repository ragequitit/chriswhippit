Murloc Gills

Quest · Westfall · Alliance
Level ~16Required –XP 460Money 2s 50c

Quest text

The corruption affecting Westfall's crops may have seeped into the ocean as well. If the local fish are affected too, it could doom Stormwind to famine. Collect gills from the murlocs along the shoreline and bring them to me. Murlocs are quite hardy, but their physiology is extremely sensitive to contaminants. Any toxins in the seawater will be readily apparent in the gills you find.
