Yelmak's Medley

Quest · Orgrimmar · Horde
Level ~60Required –XP 110

Quest text

Yelmak doesn't want his apprentice breaking anything. Well, anything more than he already has. He told me the young mixer in training has too much pride to ask for help when he really needs it. Yelmak wants to give him a taste of a particular home-brewed recipe. Gather a mix of cattail and speargrass cuttings taken from the plants growing around the water in the Valley of Honor. I'd get them myself, but if I left the shop, Whuut would surely know we're catering to his mood.
