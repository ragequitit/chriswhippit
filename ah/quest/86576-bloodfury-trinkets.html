Bloodfury Trinkets

Quest · Stonetalon Mountains · Horde
Level ~22Required –XP 2100Money 20s

Quest text

In addition to spreading their filth across the Charred Vale, the Bloodfury harpies gather anything shiny that they can find. I'm told that they collect a variety of golden-yellow sunstones for decoration. I have no use for such trinkets, but Mor'rogal has mentioned using them before. While you're fighting the harpies of the Vale, keep your eye out for Glittering Sunstones. Bring any that you find to Mor'rogal, up on the hills above us.

Rewards

  • Harpy Claw Ring
