Carnage

Quest · Wetlands · Alliance · Elite
Level ~26Required –XP 3050

Quest text

The natural order of this land is breaking. Once it was that the Razormaw raptors kept the land from overgrowth. They now live in fear of their alpha, named only for the carnage it leaves behind. I fear the beast has been driven mad by the sickness that we have yet to fully excise. It will respond to the malignant flesh of the diseased bears below Raptor Ridge, to the northeast. Leave their entrails in its cave lair. Carnage will come. Bring me its head.
