The Fugitive

Quest · Ahn'Qiraj · Horde & Alliance
Level ~60Required –Money 45s

Quest text

I've come to this timeline in search of a dangerous agent of the void. This creature has wreaked havoc across their own timeline and I fear that they will do the same here. I've followed a trail of voidstuff to this place but the trail has gone cold, which is why I'd like to enlist your aid. Nearby, there is a camp of Twilight's Hammer cultists. This creature has a way of influencing susceptible minds, so keep an eye out for anything that would be out of place, even for deranged cultist types.
