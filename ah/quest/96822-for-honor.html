For Honor

Quest · Durotar · Horde
Level ~9Required –XP 480

Quest text

We lost many brave warriors of the Horde when we sacked Tiragarde Keep. Now that the humans have reinforced the ruins, we cannot collect their remains to properly honor their deaths. Go to the ruins surrounding the keep, and search for the weapons left by our fallen kin. We will display them in a place of honor.
