Rotgath Stonebeard

Quest text

Have you ever met Rotgath Stonebeard? He is a dwarven blacksmith in Ironforge. Not the most chatty fellow around, but always reliable. We often come to him when we need bulk crafts of simpler designs--the kind that Stondry is too proud to take on himself. His turnaround times are excellent, particularly when it comes to helping new craftspeople get their shops established. I would trust no other to get your sign made fast. Let him know I sent you if he gives you any trouble!
