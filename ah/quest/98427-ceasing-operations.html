Quest text

The shredder one of the pages speaks of--I have seen them before in my travels. They can decimate a forest in a fraction of the time that hand and axe can. Entire glades--homes to the Earth Mother's children for millennia--erased in days. We will not allow this to happen in our home, a stone's throw from where our young ones take their first steps. <name>, I must ask you to gather others, go back into the mine to the northeast, and ensure it fells not a single tree more.
