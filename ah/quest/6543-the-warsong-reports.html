Quest text

Objective: Open the Bundle of Reports. Take the Warsong Reports to the Warsong Scout, Warsong Runner, and Warsong Outrider. Bring back the updates they give you to Kadrak at the northern watch tower in the barrens.

I recently deployed a few scouts into Ashenvale and I need a runner to take them their orders, and bring me back a report of what they have observed. Your first stop should be the Zoram'gar Outpost, along the coast to the west, at the Zoram Strand, to locate the first runner. You also need to stop at Splintertree Post, to the north, and along the road to the east, near Azshara. You will find a scout and an outrider there. Give them each a report, and from them, get an update on their findings.
