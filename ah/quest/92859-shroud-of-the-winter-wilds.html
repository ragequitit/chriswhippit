Shroud of the Winter Wilds

Quest · Winterspring · Horde & Alliance
Level ~59Required –XP 370

Quest text

Folks have been trying to get onto Mount Hyjal since the end of the war, but there's one big problem... all the demons in Darkwhisper Gorge! I think I have the solution. A concoction made from locally-sourced ingredients might be able to make the drinker blend in with the Winterspring surroundings so that creatures here can't see you as easily. I'd love to test this theory, if you're game for bringing me a few small things!
