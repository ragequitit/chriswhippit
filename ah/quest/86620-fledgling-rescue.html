Quest text

Though we have a good line of gryphons here, there are always a few egg clutches in the wild that are in danger of not making it. A gryphon parent can lose a fight against a wolf, or fall sick. Without help, the fledglings will never survive. Fortunately, we have the means to raise and protect lost young ones like this. If you find lost eggs in the wild, bring them to me.
