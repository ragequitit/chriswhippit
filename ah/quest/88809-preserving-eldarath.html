Preserving Eldarath

Quest · Azshara · Alliance
Level ~56Required –XP 5100Money 1g 27s

Quest text

Millennia ago, Eldarath was a sight to behold; a living testament to the glory and might of the Kaldorei. What we call Eldarath today is hardly a pile of rubble by comparison. Nonetheless, what remains is worth salvaging, if only to preserve our history. The Spitelash must be driven out before Eldarath is completely ransacked. Following the road north east will lead you right into the heart of the ruins. Go there, and retrieve as many of our relics as you can--and their commander's head.
