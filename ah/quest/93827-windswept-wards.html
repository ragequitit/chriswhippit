Windswept Wards

Quest · Mount Hyjal · Horde & Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 6200Money 2g 11s 75c

Quest text

The mountain winds bring dark signs. Harpies nest high where our paws do not go. The gems they wear hold strong power. Bound with our charms, they guard us from curse and bind the spirits to our wards. But the climb is hard, and the harpies strike with claw and storm. If you are strong like they say, go to the high crags where the wind screams. Harpy grounds are in mid land Hyjal, we see them fly above us, in the north east. Take the charms from the sky witches and bring them to me.
