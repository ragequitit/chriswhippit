Demonic Deceptions

Quest · Winterspring · Horde & Alliance
Level ~59Required –

Quest text

De loa have told me of a poison gatherin' strength in Ashenvale, on the tainted land where the great demon Mannoroth was slain. Someone be drawin' power from the pit lord's remains. They must be usin' illusions to hide their rituals. If you are to stop them, you'll be needin' protectin from their deceptions. My tribe uses an extract from owlbeast glands in an enchantment to discern truth from lies. Collect these glands from the owlbeasts in Winterspring.
