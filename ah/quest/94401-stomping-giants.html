Stomping Giants

Quest · Un'Goro Crater · Horde · Elite
Level ~53Required –

Quest text

You might have heard of kodos, but have you heard of the stegodons? The kodo are descended from the mighty stegodons, but except for in the deep crater, the stegodon are gone! Far to the southwest, you will find a place known as Terror Run, the last place where this mighty beast still roams free. They are a nuisance, and while more likely to be prey than predators, their enormous size and thunderous stomps deter most dinos from leaving their nesting grounds. Kill a few, just to quiet things down.
