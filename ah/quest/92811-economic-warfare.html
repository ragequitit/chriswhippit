Economic Warfare

Quest · Desolace · Horde & Alliance
Level ~35Required –

Quest text

One thing that war has taught us: money is power. All centaur in Desolace have come to an agreement to barter with intricately carved wood that shares tales of a centaur's own glory and conquests. It serves as the only reprieve from battle, and only the most level-headed are permitted to engage with other clans. I have seen your kind trade in gold and silver. Worthless to us, as our totems are to you. Bring me some, and the Magram Clan will be pleased.
