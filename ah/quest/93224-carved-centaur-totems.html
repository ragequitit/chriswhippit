Carved Centaur Totems

Quest · Desolace · Alliance

Quest text

It likely comes as no surprise to you that both of the centaur clans are hesitant to accept aid from anyone outside of their clan. This is especially true for folks like you and me - you know, non-centaurs. One thing we all have in common is an understanding that money is power, and we have learned that the local centaur barter with carved wood. If you bring me a few samples of their designs, I can put in a rush order for similar etchings. Perhaps we can buy ourselves an advantage.
