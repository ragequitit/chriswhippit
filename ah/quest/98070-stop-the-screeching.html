Stop the Screeching

Quest · Thousand Needles · Horde
Level ~35Required –XP 2450Money 25s

Quest text

The Screeching Canyon is home to a local band of harpies known for their ferocious nature and raiding tactics. They have ambushed the last few supply caravans that have been brave enough to traverse this place. I fear if something isn't done soon, there will be no caravans that will dare make the trek to Freewind Post. Go, slay as many of these harpies as you are able. Return to me once the deed has been done, and I will reward you well for your efforts.
