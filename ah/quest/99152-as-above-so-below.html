As Above, So Below

Quest · Tirisfal Glades · Horde
Level ~9Required –XP 900

Quest text

While the Tarnished in Shadowvale to the southwest are definitely a problem, we cannot allow the cellars below the town to fester into a full-blown Scourge uprising on Bandarion Keep. I need to get these trainees up to snuff in case that happens. You, however, are going to receive what I like to call a crash course. Travel beneath Shadowvale and retrieve some of their wretched bones so we can study them further. I need ones that still retain some of the initial reanimation magic--that's glowing ones.
