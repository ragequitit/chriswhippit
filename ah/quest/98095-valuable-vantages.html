Valuable Vantages

Quest · Hillsbrad Foothills · Horde
Level ~32Required –XP 2000

Quest text

Our high command inserts strategic troops using bat riders. Key points around the foothills have lost their signaling beacons. Take these vials. Uncork them in three locations: the hills bordering Dun Garok, the plateau overlooking Dunholde Keep, and the last on the ridge of the Headland above the town of Hillsbrad. The vapors that emanate will be like a potent tuning rod to the senses of the bat, drawing them to the desired drop-off points once more. Look for crushed empty vials and renew our marks.
