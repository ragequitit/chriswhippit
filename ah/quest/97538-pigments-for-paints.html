Pigments for Paints

Quest · Stonetalon Mountains · Horde
Level ~22Required –XP 2100Money 20s

Quest text

Greetings, friend. I wonder if I may ask a favor of you? I have just run out of red pigment for my paints, and I am looking for one with younger hooves to make the long journey into the Stonetalon Mountains on my behalf. In the middle of the mountain pass there lies a large circular lake, Mirkfallon Lake, that has a unique plant that grows within its waters. The pods of this plant, mirkweed, turn a vibrant red when dried. Gather the pods of this plant for me, and I will reward you well for your efforts.
