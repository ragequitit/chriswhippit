Transmuted Tincture

Quest · Azshara · Horde & Alliance
Level ~56Required –XP 3950

Quest text

I have transmuted the firewater into a tincture which I believe will be the key to undoing the Legashi's corruption. Eager as I am to ease the suffering of the Blackmaw, it must be tested. I have seen many of the coursers and skystormers east of Ursolan suffering from the satyr's corruption. They will serve as our test subjects. Go, and test my would-be cure on the afflicted beasts. Before giving the tincture the beast must be weakened to ensure it can be administered with little resistance.
