Quest text

Now that you've arrived in Valanaar, you'll likely want to check in with the other members of the Windshapers here. The current Grand Skyseer and her people operate out of the lodge near the center of Valanaar. You should also be aware, the Windshapers and High Order don't get along very well in these parts. Be careful and give the High Order a good, wide berth. We've got enough to worry about with the cult, I won't tolerate faction violence in the streets of Valanaar.
