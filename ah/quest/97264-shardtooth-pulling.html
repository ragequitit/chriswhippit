Shardtooth Pulling

Quest · Winterspring · Horde
Level ~59Required –XP 6200Money 1g 70s 50c

Quest text

The Shardtooth are becoming more rabid every season. They compete with each other and the predators of the wilds for what limited food sources they can reach. More often, they attack us, hunger driving their aggression in the bitter cold. The bears are numerous and strong-willed. With their numbers reduced, the families that remain will grow stronger than ever. Help them on their way. Cull the eldest of them.
