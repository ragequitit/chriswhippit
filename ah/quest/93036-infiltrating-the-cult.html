Infiltrating the Cult

Quest · Zephras Isle · Horde & Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 70

Quest text

You may or may not be aware of this, but Sania Silverstream over there is a "missionary" for the Al'Aketh. They like to play at being a normal religious order and send non-threatening emissaries around to recruit. We've tolerated Sania so far to avoid direct conflict with the cult, but it's obvious the illusion of peace won't last much longer. You are new here and unknown to most, so I'd like to see if you can use Sania to get close to the cult and find out what their plans are for the village.
