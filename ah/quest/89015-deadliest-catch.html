Deadliest Catch

Quest · Azshara · Alliance · Elite
Level ~56Required –XP 5800

Quest text

If ye fancy yourself a real angler, there's bigger fish out here than ye can catch with a rod. Mammoth Sharks lurk in the deepest waters of the northern an' southern coasts. They swim far out, right before the sea floor drops away into the great ocean. They're mighty big; I'd advise ye take a group of anglers with ye. Dwarf like me? I prefer dry land. Those sharks'd swallow me in one bite. If ye do go hunting one, bring me a cut o' the meat. I'd be mighty thankful.
