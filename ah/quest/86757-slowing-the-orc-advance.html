Quest text

Orcs have taken over the eastern forest, and their scouts and grunts now lurk throughout the woods! It's not safe for our kind to move about Ashenvale any more. We need to push back the orcs and secure the forest. We've been able to pick off some of the grunts and scouts by carefully sneaking around the edges of their camp. Fight the orcs and bring me the jagged swords and curved knives that they use. I'll make sure their weapons are salvaged and repurposed, so nothing goes to waste.
