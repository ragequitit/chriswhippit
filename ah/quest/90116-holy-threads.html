Holy Threads

Quest · New Avalon · Horde & Alliance
Level ?Required –

Quest text

This loom is special. Did you know that? All of our craftsmen had their tools of the trade blessed by our high priests. In particular, the loom attracts stray fabrics and thread destined to be used in the holiest of our ceremonial vestments. Over time, we will have enough gathered to create a new robe, and these days are a cause for celebration, for me at least. If you are interested in the process for your own curiosity, thread some bolts near the loom and watch the Light at work.
