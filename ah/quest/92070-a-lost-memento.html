A Lost Memento

Quest · Blackmaw Hold · Horde & Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 11300

Quest text

The necklace is adorned with miscellaneous charms: scraps of feathers, acorn shells, and colorful pebbles. Its leather strap is torn. The ornamentation suggests it belonged to a Furbolg, while its small size and rough quality indicate a child's handiwork. There's a chance the necklace is of some sentimental value to one of the Blackmaw. It may be good to find the owner and return it.
