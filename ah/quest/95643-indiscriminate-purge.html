Indiscriminate Purge

Quest · Riverglades · Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 3150

Quest text

As Lady Amberhall's apprentice in the dark arts, I may not look it but I'm usually quite capable of handling things on my own. I did not, however, anticipate an entire cave of void-infused ogres. If you managed to get all the way down here without being captured, you must be powerful yourself. I was not so lucky with those ogres and their masters roaming about. Do the world a favor and kill the hideous beasts. Nothing good comes from a fanatic with a purpose, empowered or otherwise.
