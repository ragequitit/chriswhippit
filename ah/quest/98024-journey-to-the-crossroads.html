Journey to the Crossroads

Quest · The Barrens · Horde
Level ~18Required –XP 460Money 3s

Quest text

After journeying here from the elemental plane, the problems our lands face may seem small. Rest assured, the dangers the horde faces are very real, and you will grow formidable should you survive your service. If you'd like to get started doing some good in our world, travel southwest to the vast lands known as the Barrens. There is a settlement there known as the Crossroads. The proud citizens there are ever in need of aid. Go with honor, adventurer!
