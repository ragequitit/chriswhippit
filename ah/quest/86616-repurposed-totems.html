Repurposed Totems

Quest · The Hinterlands · Alliance
Level ~48Required –XP 4400Money 83s 50c

Quest text

As a shaman, one of the challenges of communing with the elements is that there are other shaman with opposing agendas. The elements do not care why we do what we do, they just do as we ask! The trolls at Jintha'alor also practice the shamanic arts, and their interruptions and attacks are a constant thorn to us here. Find their Vilebranch totems and bring them back to me so that I can nullify their powers. Some they carry, some they place as wards around their altar.
