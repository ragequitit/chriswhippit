Quest text

Objective: Bring the Filled Dalson's Tears Bottle to Shadow Priestess Vandis at the Bulwark, Western Plaguelands.

You carefully fill the bottle with enough of the cauldron's ichors for what you consider to be suitably sized sample. With the poison in hand, it is time to return to Shadow Priestess Vandis at the Bulwark to complete the mission.
