Silithid Intrusion

Quest · Un'Goro Crater · Horde
Level ~53Required –

Quest text

Though Un'Goro's ecosystem is stable and would survive all but a crashing meteor, there is one encroaching danger that threatens everyone and everything in the crater: the Silithid. All it takes is one stray bug to start a hive, and sure enough, you can find ruptured ground leading into slithering tunnels along the southern mountains. These creatures are vicious, and they spread like a plague. They're too close to the ravasaur grounds and must be culled.
