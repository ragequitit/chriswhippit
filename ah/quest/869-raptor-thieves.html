Quest text

Objective: Bring 12 Raptor Heads to Gazrog at the Crossroads.

Not long ago, a shipment of silver was stolen from our guard tower. It was meant as payroll to the Crossroads' guards, and we want that silver back. The strange thing is... we caught one of the thieves on the night of the theft. And... it was a raptor! Unbelievable! I don't know what raptors would want with silver. But I don't care -- I want the raptors dead so they won't steal from us again! Hunt raptors in the Barrens. Collect for me their heads!
