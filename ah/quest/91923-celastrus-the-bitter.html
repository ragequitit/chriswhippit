Celastrus the Bitter

Quest · Mount Hyjal · Horde & Alliance · Elite
Level ?Required –XP 7550

Quest text

The mountain is overrun with wild growth, inflamed by the fel energies that rained down so long ago. Now the weeds and plants are angry, corrupted, poisoned. There is a great thorn-bush called Celastrus that lurks about the slopes to the east. It kills animals, lashes out at other plants, feeds on the bodies of the dead. It must be destroyed! The weeds must be cleared so that the mountain may regrow.
