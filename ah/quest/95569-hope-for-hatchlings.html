Hope for Hatchlings

Quest · Mount Hyjal · Horde & Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 5800Money 1g 45s

Quest text

The owls are under threat by the fel corruption that afflicts the mountainside, and the creatures poisoned and enraged by those energies. Many owls have abandoned their nests for safer places higher up the mountain. Others have fallen in defense of their young. For the owls to have a future, their eggs must be protected and their fledglings nurtured. Bring the abandoned eggs to me. You may find eggs in abandoned nests at the base of trees north and east of here.
