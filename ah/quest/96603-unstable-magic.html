Quest text

Archmage Rhonin is presently abroad investigating a disturbance in the flow of magic across Azeroth. The Archmage has charged me with soliciting the aid of strong adventurers like yourself in gathering important field data to aid him. Take this Arcane Censer. While afflicted by its magic, you will be able to extract motes of unstable magic from the most skilled spell casters and other singularly powerful entities you may encounter. Bring me said motes and the Kirin Tor will be in your debt.
