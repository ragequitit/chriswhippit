I have cutting to do. I can't tell you what I'm going to cut, but... I need myself a blade. A certain blade... Some people call it a moonsteel broadsword. I call it a swing blade. You help me? Maybe you find it in town, maybe you talk to a blacksmith and he'll make it special. But don't sleep on this. What I'm cutting won't keep forever...
