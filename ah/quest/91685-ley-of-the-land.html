Ley of the Land

Quest · Azshara · Horde · Elite
Level ~56Required –XP 6800Money 1g 65s

Quest text

When we first got here, I did some surveying for any untapped resources. Y'know, typical prospector stuff. I didn't see nothin' special 'til I searched Lake Mennar, to the south. There, I saw a huge rift floating above the water's surface. I couldn't get close, but I think it was an open leyline. If I'm right, we can use this wand to siphon magic straight from the source! Bottle that up, sell it to the highest bidder, and bam! I'm rich! If you go and siphon that rift for me, I'll cut you in.
