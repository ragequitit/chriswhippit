Quest text

Hailen last said she was only going to survey the north reaches, so if it was her you found, there must be more to the story. There is a high elf, Adon, that resides northwest of the ruins of Northdale, near the road to Quel'Thalas. He might have a better understanding of the elven movements through these territories. I'll ensure we have a burial for this soul, whomever it may be.
