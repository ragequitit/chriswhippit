An Enticing Offer

Quest · Elwynn Forest · Alliance
Level ~9Required –XP 625

Quest text

I've got a few ideas on how to deal with murlocs. And one of those also helps some others in the process. There's a weed often found on farms in the area called duskweed, and in large quantities the petals are toxic to most animals. If we make some murloc bait with the plant, hopefully that can train those creatures to stay away. And some farmers get their fields cleared in the process. Truly a win-win... truly a win-win.
