Quest text

Clearly these wayward ogres are no friends to the Twilight's Hammer. We orcs have an honored history fighting alongside ogres, and it may be time to call upon those old alliances again. And this head may be the key. There have been reports of ogre activity in the hills far to the north. If this Clubtoe is to be believed, that must be where this clan calls home. Go to their stronghold and offer this trophy as a token of our friendship. With luck, they will listen to your proposal before they step on you.
