In Memoriam

Quest · Riverglades · Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 2150

Quest text

If you don't mind, I'd like you to do the honor of lighting these memorial candles. Go to the cemetery just outside and place a fresh candle wherever you see one that's gone out. Such a task is a far cry from the excitement of your other adventures, I'm sure. Think of this as an opportunity for service and reflection--a quiet moment to acknowledge those who left this world before us, so that we might carry on and make it a better place.
