Quest text

Ever strike a deal with a satyr, mon? I have. How could I resist? The promise of power, knowledge far exceeding what any ordinary tome could offer... all alongside an eager and charismatic minion. What more could I ask for? Bah, of course they would never bend the knee to a mortal. I bargained when I had nothing, only to be cast aside with even less. Now, I want revenge. Revenge against the Hatefury. Raid their camp to the northeast! Stain the dirt with their foul blood.
