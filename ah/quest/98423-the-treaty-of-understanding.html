The Treaty of Understanding

Quest · Dun Morogh · Alliance
Level ~9Required –XP 2550Money 16s

Quest text

Inside of the vault you find a tablet detailing the terms of a peace treaty between the Bronzebeard and Wildhammer dwarf clans. It appears to date back to the time period just after the War of the Three Hammers, and is very likely a notable piece of dwarven history. The king of Ironforge would no doubt be interested in this artifact.
