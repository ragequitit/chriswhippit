Quest text

The satyr within the Legashi stronghold are even more fearsome than those of the outer camps. When our party was discovered, we found ourselves overwhelmed. One of us was slain, and another captured. I was the only one able to successfully retreat. I cannot return to Rageroar with my head hung in shame, but I cannot fight the Legashi alone, either. I ask you, then, to assist me in weakening the satyr's forces. Go into their stronghold and slay them.
