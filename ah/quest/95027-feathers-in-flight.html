Feathers in Flight

Quest · Mount Hyjal · Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 6000

Quest text

We always need more arrows for hunting and fighting, and for feathers, we use the white feathers of the Hyjal owl. They're naturally fluffy and soft, but I stiffen them up with a mixture designed to make them perfect for fletching. You can get feathers from the owls, of course, but they also leave behind feathers in their nests, which you can find all over to the south of here. Bring back a bundle of feathers, all right?
