The State of the Mines

Quest · Westfall · Alliance
Level ~16Required –XP 490Money 3s

Quest text

The well samples you collected contain toxic metals! If we do not put an end to this corruption quickly, it will be decades before crops can grow in this soil again. The Defias have seized the mines of Westfall and use creatures as labor to strip their resources. Disrupt them by slaying their miners in the Jangolode Mine to the north, and the Gold Coast Quarry to the west.
