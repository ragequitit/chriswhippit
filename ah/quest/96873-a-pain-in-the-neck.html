A Pain in the Neck

Quest · Durotar · Horde
Level ~9Required –XP 525Money 1s 10c

Quest text

The trolls east of here have lost their will to the witchdoctor, Zalazane. He uses many cruel magics to keep hold of his prisoners' minds, but one of his favorite tricks is to make them wear pendants wrapped in evil binding spells. Practice your enchanting skills by stripping the magic from these pendants, and bring me the results. And don't accidentally put one around your neck, if you value your freedom!
