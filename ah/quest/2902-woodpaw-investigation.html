Objective: Find the gnoll battle plans somewhere in the gnoll camps to the south of Camp Mojache.

If we were to find those plans, then we'd have a clearer idea of what is making them so worked up. I want you to head back to the Woodpaw and find anything that might resemble their battle plans.
