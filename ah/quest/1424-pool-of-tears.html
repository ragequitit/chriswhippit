Quest text

Objective: Fel'zerul in Stonard wants you to gather 10 Atal'ai Artifacts.

The Warchief sent us to investigate strange happenings at The Temple of Atal'Hakkar. The Atal'ai trolls are a tribe of dark casters, known for practicing forbidden magic. You can imagine how astonished we were when we arrived here to discover the temple had been sunk. The green dragons now guard the site, claiming to be protectors of Azeroth. We need to discover what happened. Explore around the Pool of Tears and gather Atal'ai artifacts. From those I can begin to piece together this puzzle.
