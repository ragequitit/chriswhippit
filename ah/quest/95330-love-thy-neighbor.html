Love Thy Neighbor

Quest · Riverglades · Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 290

Quest text

My husband and I have a farm north of here. I come to Farholde after each harvest to sell what we grow. In good seasons, I like to set aside whatever we don't sell to give to folks who need a little pick-me-up. Gotta take care of your neighbors, I say. There's a logging camp north of our farm, right on the river. I'm sure those boys could use some fruits and vegetables to give 'em some variety in their diet. If you're heading that way, could you give this to the foreman there? Name's Halligan Turner.
