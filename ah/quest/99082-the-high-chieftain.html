The High Chieftain

Quest · Mulgore · Horde
Level ~8Required –XP 90

Quest text

Your bravery defending your people demands recognition. Journey to Thunder Bluff to the northwest, our mighty city whose shadow grants us shade in the warmer months, and seek audience with my father. It was he who led the charge with the young Warchief Thrall to reclaim these plains and drive the centaur away for good. As High Chieftain, he will reward you generously for your part in ensuring his own bravery is honored. You must take a lift to reach the High Chieftain, who sits on the central rise.
