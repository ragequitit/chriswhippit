Crocs of the Sky

Quest · Wetlands · Alliance
Level ~26Required –XP 2200

Quest text

You're quite the go-getter! I have just one more favor to ask, if you're up for something a bit more dangerous. The crocolisk trade is steady money, but they're small fish compared to what whelp scales will bring. Folks will pay top dollar for a sack of those. Crocs of the sky, I call 'em! There's no way my apprentice could get them--but it seems right up your alley! You'll find whelps far to the east, along the Green Belt. But be careful, there's nasty orcs in those hills!
