    Quest text

    Objective: Bring 10 Dense Grinding Stones, 10 Solid Grinding Stones and 10 Heavy Grinding Stones to Vish Kozus, Captain of the Guard at Cenarion Hold in Silithus. You must also bring Logistics Task Briefing III in order to complete this quest.

    The Cenarion Hold guard is in short supply of grinding stones. Procure a batch and deliver them immediately to Captain Vish Kozus in Cenarion Hold.
