Data Hoarders

Quest · Dun Morogh · Alliance
Level ~9Required –XP 625

Quest text

Before we fled Gnomeregan, I made a hard copy of my family's archives. Arcanotechnical field equations, Second War submarine hull designs, Grandma Quikswitch's chili recipe... our greatest accomplishments are in those files. It wasn't until we reached Ironforge that I found a hole in my pack! Several punch cards are gone, and without them the data's too scrambled to recover. The cards must be buried in the snow, somewhere outside Gnomeregan. Could you find them? I know it's a long shot...
