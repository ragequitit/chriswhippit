Chakuyak

Quest · Mulgore · Horde
Level ~8Required –XP 340

Quest text

Among the prairie wolves that hunt the hills nearby is one that is especially aggressive. We have taken to calling her Chakuyak. She has become a threat to travelers coming from abroad. She stalks the trees west of Stonebull Lake, moving from tree stand to tree stand. End this threat and bring me her pelt, and I will speak your praises to the chief. Beware, for she is dangerous! You might see other hunters seeking her out there, and perhaps you could work as a team.
