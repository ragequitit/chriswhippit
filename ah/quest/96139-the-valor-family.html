The Valor Family

Quest · Duskwood · Alliance
Level ~28Required –XP 1400

Quest text

In dark times like these, it's important we remember our heroes as much as our foes. Darkshire would not still be standing if it weren't for the bravery of folk like the good Lady Althea or those who lost their lives at Raven Hill. Have you read much on Raven Hill? No, of course not. Its history was wiped out along with its people. My library, impressive as it is, has little on the subject. If you happen to find anything, I'd appreciate it if you brought it here. Those folk deserve to be remembered.
