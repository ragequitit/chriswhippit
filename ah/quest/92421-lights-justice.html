Quest text

As a paladin of the Forsaken, it is my duty to exact the will of our Dark Lady while tending to our people's needs. We are fortunate with an opportunity to accomplish both. Above the Undercity, the scourge still roam unchecked in great numbers. Our forces dwindle trying to reclaim our land, and decaying flesh cannot be reknit. Not without replacements. Would that our glorious captain Garek Bandarion still be with us, I know what he would wish. To finish what he started and heal our kin. For Lordaeron!
