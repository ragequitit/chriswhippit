The Conjuring

Quest · The Barrens · Horde · Elite
Level ~18Required –

Quest text

The demon, Des'Altek, seems to have broken free of it's prison, but its still bound to it like a prisoner to their cell. We must summon forth the demon to capture its soul again. To the north, in Ashenvale, where great demons fell, there is a location on the ridge that was once used by the Legion for sacrifices. The ground is permanently scarred from the atrocities that occurred here, but if we can gather the Blood of the Legion from the demons that still infest this land... that's the key.
