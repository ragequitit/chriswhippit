Ward Restocking

Quest · Arathi Highlands · Horde
Level ~39Required –XP 2800Money 20s

Quest text

Apothecary Jorell was supposed to bring us a case of antidotes and elixirs days ago, but we're still empty handed. That fool has probably poisoned himself again by brewing in that poorly-ventilated shack he calls his lab. If you want to further help our ward, you would be a far more reliable individual for this job. Follow the road to Go'shek farm, south and a bit west of here. You'll likely find it packed and ready to go, with Jorell either missing or unconcerned with his obligations.
