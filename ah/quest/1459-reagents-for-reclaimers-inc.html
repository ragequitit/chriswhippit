Quest text

Objective: Bring 7 vials of Scorpashi Venom and 3 Aged Kodo Hides to Kreldig Ungor in Desolace.

Next on Leftwitch's list is... let's see... hmm. Oh, here it is. Simple enough, but pretty explicit in his directions. He needs venom from the scorpashi, and hide from some kodo. The fine print says both are from specific creatures: the venom's got to come from any of the scorpashi, and the hide's got to come from an aged kodo, not the dying or ancient ones. Mages, I tell ya, some of the stuff they want... it's just weird.
