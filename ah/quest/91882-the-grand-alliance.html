The Grand Alliance

Quest · Blasted Lands · Horde & Alliance
Level ~55Required –XP 550

Quest text

Years ago, the Horde and the Alliance put aside their differences to fight a greater threat--the Legion. On Mount Hyjal, the Grand Alliance forces battled Archimonde and his minions to a halt. The retreat from the mountain was perilous, however, and some brave fighters were left behind. Now that the way up the great mountain is again open, perhaps some of these heroes remain alive in the wilds? Go to Mount Hyjal and brave its dangers. Find what became of our forces there.
