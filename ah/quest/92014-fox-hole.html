Fox Hole

Quest · Mount Hyjal · Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 6000

Quest text

Every time I take my attention off the supplies I've lugged up here for more than a few minutes, one of nature's finest thieves is pilfering and rummaging through it. The damned foxes seem to have made a burrow beneath the boulders nearby. Apparently, me digging out this hole gave them an idea of their own. I'm working on a solution. Tell you what, bring back some of their fox tails. That'll teach them a lesson, and I'll hang the tails around the perimeter to scare the others off!
