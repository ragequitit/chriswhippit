Objective: Speak to Elling Trias in Stormwind.

That Marzon would be contacting a member of the Defias and have ties to Lescovar... That is not even considering the fact that Lescovar is a noble and well connected. It effectively makes him above the law. You'll need other help. I loathe to do so, but there is someone I know that will aid us. You'll find Trias in the Trade District of the city.
