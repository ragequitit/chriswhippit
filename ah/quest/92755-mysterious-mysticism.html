Mysterious Mysticism

Quest · Desolace · Horde & Alliance
Level ~35Required –XP 2050Money 18s

Quest text

With each passing day, our scavengers return with fewer and fewer armaments from fallen adventurers. While we cannot use these pieces directly, we do salvage them for scraps. The most magical of the armaments go to me, as I can disenchant them and make use of the mysterious remnants to enchant our weapons, particularly those of our windchasers. I am in need of Greater Mystic Essences if I am to supply our next raiding party with worthwhile equipment.
