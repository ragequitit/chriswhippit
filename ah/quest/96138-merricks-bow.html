Quest text

A bow left by the spirit of Merrick Valor, famed Watcher who prided himself on keeping the roads safe from encroaching enemies. With Merrick's spirit still wandering the roads, perhaps his unfinished business was to clear out invaders near his home in Raven Hill. The ogres south of town may be what's troubling his spirit.
