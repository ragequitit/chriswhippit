Slay the Spitelash

Quest · Azshara · Alliance
Level ~56Required –XP 4400Money 83s 50c

Quest text

Azshara may appear beautiful, but it is a cursed place. There are horrors deep beneath its waters, waiting to emerge and tear the world asunder. All we can do for now is fight the horrors that have already surfaced. The Spitelash Naga occupy the Ruins of Eldarath to the north east. The ones within the ruins are well-fortified, so we must pick off the weaker ones along the outskirts first. As you can see, we could always use another fighter here, if you are up to the task.
