Quest text

Timear and I have been scrying the area on Kalimdor known as Alcaz Island and we've all but confirmed the presence of a powerful magic user there. The mage in question was able to dispel our probes, but before she did, I was able to glean an image of a tome of some sort. I believe it was a journal, written in ancient draconic script. I don't know what is in that book, but I'd like to study it. We want you to travel to the hidden prison beneath Alcaz Island and recover that book for us.
