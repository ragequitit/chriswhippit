Book Return

Quest · Elwynn Forest · Alliance · Elite
Level ~9Required –XP 675Money 2s 50c

Quest text

While you're investigating the Fargodeep Mine, see if you can find any of Brother Paxton's books to return. In particular, Paxton is very concerned about a book titled, "Fun with Elementals". He thinks even a kobold could use it to summon an elemental servant. Be warned, the depths of the mine are fairly dangerous. You should bring friends. Take this book bag to protect the books when you find them!
