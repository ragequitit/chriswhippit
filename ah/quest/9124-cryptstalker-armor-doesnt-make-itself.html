Quest text

Objective: Huntsman Leopold at Light's Hope Chapel in the Eastern Plaguelands wants 30 Crypt Fiend Parts.

How would you like to earn yourself one of these shiny coins? <Leopold holds up an insignia of the Crusade.> I've got a little problem that you might be able to help me with, <name>. Bring me back a bundle of parts and I'll pay you with an insignia - usable at the quartermaster.
