Field to Clear

Quest · The Barrens · Horde
Level ~18Required –XP 1850

Quest text

I'm so glad you saw me! The weather was lovely this morning, so I walked from Camp Taurajo down the golden road. When it came time to head back, I couldn't--a pack of hyenas started circling, and Stormhide lizards blocked my only escape. Scrambling up this tree I went, and now there are too many foes about at this hour for me to make the run home through the fields. Would you mind clearing out these Stormhide and Hecklefang Stalkers for me?
