Grund and Gozwin

Quest · Anvilmar · Horde & Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 410

Quest text

Oi, had me back turned gettin' the boar roast ready when I heard me pal Gozwin screamin' fer his life! Wicked fangs and a blur are all I saw when I turned. Such huge paws! A snow leopard was draggin' its gnomish catch away. I grabbed me shotgun and fired above the beast. It dropped him and sprinted off. Luckily, it hadn't done more than give ol' Goz some deep scratches. Head back to camp in the northwestern hills and retrieve Gozwin's Mechanic's Log. Kill that snow leopard too if you see it!
