The Broken Glaive

Quest · Dire Maul · Horde & Alliance
Level ~60Required –XP 1450Money 1g 98s

Quest text

The air smells sharp and metallic as you reach down to inspect the corpse of Prince Tortheldrin. Extremely powerful magic was just dispelled here. You notice that the corpse before you looks old and weathered, lacking the vitality that the prince exuded before he fell. There are other physical differences as well. One of Tortheldrin's glaives lies near his body, broken in two. The Shen'dralar Ancients will want to know of what you observed here.
