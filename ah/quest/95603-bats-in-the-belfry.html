Bats in the Belfry

Quest · Riverglades · Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 2150

Quest text

Since brother Lowley and I began serving Farholde in a more secular capacity, the church here has fallen into disuse. I'd like to think that once the fighting is over, we will be able to bring the Light back to this place. Although, I fear that may not be for a long while... Still, we need to keep the church in working order, in the hope that day will come. To that end, a colony of bats has taken to roosting in the upper stories of the church. It would be a great help if you could remove them.
