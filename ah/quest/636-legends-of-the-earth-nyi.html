Level 35Required 30XP 280

Quest text

Objective: Find the Shards of Myzrael.

This book tells the tale of an elemental princess, Myzrael, and of her imprisonment deep underneath the Arathi Highlands. It also tells of a secret outcropping of crystals in the highlands. These crystals, the Shards of Myzrael, are a window to the princess' prison. If a brave soul found the Shards of Myzrael, the perhaps he could speak with her...
