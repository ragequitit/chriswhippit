Red is Not Dead

Quest · New Avalon · Horde & Alliance
Level ?Required –

Quest text

Hm, speaking of secrets, I have been working on a new project: luxury packs for our highest ranking soldiers. They must be large, crafted from the finest silks, and most importantly, as red as the crest of the Scarlet Crusade. The material is the easy part, I intend to use your dawnwoven threads for this. Finding the perfect dye, however, is well beyond my capabilities. Scour the world in search of the perfect red hue and I'll consider you a partner in this endeavor by sharing my final design with you.
