WANTED: Jai'vhanel

Quest · Darkshore · Alliance
Level ~16Required –XP 1150

Quest text

Attention! A reward is being offered for the death of the owl "Jai'vhanel" also known as "Swordfeather". Unfortunately this owl has gone mad and has begun to attack travelers as they journey along the road near Auberdine. Jai'vhanel was last seen north of the Ameth'Aran ruins near the mountain base. Bring back one of her jet black feathers as proof of her death. Reward claims for this bounty should be claimed with Sentinel Glynda Nal'Shea in Auberdine. The Auberdine Village Council
