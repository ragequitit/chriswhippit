Azeroth is a large world, and the territory of the Horde is vast. The Undercity, Orgrimmar, and Thunder Bluff all have very unique people and culture. If you are to be a guest of the Horde here on Azeroth, it would do you good to visit each of our capital cities in turn. Speak with my advisor Nazgrel for directions.
