A Taste of Home

Quest · City of Dalaran · Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 5100Money 1g 55s

Quest text

Are you familiar with Silvermoon Shimmerwine? It has a delicate flavor that is entirely unique to the vineyards of Eversong and it is the one thing I truly miss from my homeland. Unfortunately, it is extremely hard to find on this side of the Thalassian Gate. I had a patron bring me a bottle that he claimed to have "liberated" from blood elves in northern Azshara. If your adventures take you to that region and you can recover more bottles for me, I would handsomely reward your efforts.

Rewards

  • Everlasting Silvermoon Shimmerwine
