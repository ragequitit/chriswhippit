Out in the Field

Quest · Riverglades · Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 290

Quest text

There is a watch tower overlooking the plains to the north. It serves as an observation point to track any motion of the Horde's forces. The watcher stationed there is named Cornelius. Speak to him and get a status report. He is all alone out there, so I like to check on him with some regularity. Fair warning, he can be a bit...taciturn. It's why he was assigned such a remote post in the first place.
