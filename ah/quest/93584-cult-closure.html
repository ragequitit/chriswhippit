Quest text

The lands surrounding the Well of Eternity are overrun with followers of the Wild King. Many once stood beside us, bound by the same oaths, but their devotion has curdled into reckless zeal. They actively hinder our efforts to tend Nordrassil and protect the Well. I will not pretend this is easy, nor that my hands will be clean. Still, we cannot allow them to harm our land or strike our flank when the greater incursion begins. Thin their ranks swiftly, and may Elune forgive what necessity demands.
