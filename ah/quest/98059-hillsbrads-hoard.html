Hillsbrad's Hoard

Quest · Hillsbrad Foothills · Alliance
Level ~32Required –XP 2200Money 11s

Quest text

Hillsbrad's council hasn't answered our new magistrate's letters. The last word I pried out of a passing merchant from the town did little to justify their silence. We're only asking for a small platoon--yet I'm told multiple siege ballista rest in pristine condition within a walled-off compound. Walk into their town hall like a citizen returning a census request, and place this neatly upon the bookkeeping table. Return to me when the deed is done.
