Quest text

A large, broken trap lies before you. From the looks of it, the ogres have tried to repair the trap but to no avail. A hastily written note lies next to the trap, and strangely enough it details exactly what is needed to repair it: a thorium widget and some frost oil. Were the trap repaired, an ogre passing near it might get trapped, and could be avoided. Clearly, it would seem fortuitous that very few ogres know how to read. Clearly.
