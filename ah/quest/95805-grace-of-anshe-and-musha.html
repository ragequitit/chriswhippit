Grace of An'she and Mu'sha

Quest · Red Cloud Mesa · Horde
Level ~4Required –XP 360

Quest text

The sun and the moon, An'she and Mu'sha, are not part of our world like the elements, yet their light gives us life, guidance, and hope. We pay respect to the spirits of the world, and so we pay respect to the twins as well. If you would show your respect, take this smoldering incense and bring it to a shrine in the hills to the southeast, overlooking the mesa, south of the quilboar. Be quick, so that you can reach the shrine before the incense burns away. Don't get it wet!

Rewards

  • Scorched Leather Pouch
