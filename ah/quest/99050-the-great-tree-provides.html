The Great Tree Provides

Quest · Teldrassil · Alliance
Level ~9Required –XP 850Money 85c

Quest text

I will need your help acquiring the ingredients I need to stop the affliction long enough for Eralya to make it back here for further treatment. Thankfully, our living home is still bountiful and will provide in our time of need. The core ingredient is dewy lasher fronds. You'll find them around Lake Al'Ameth directly south of here, or Wellspring Lake and its river to the northwest. In addition, I'll need an empty vial and some spring water. Both can be bought here in town, or traded for.
