Supplies and Studies

Quest · Badlands · Horde & Alliance
Level ~40Required –XP 3150Money 25s

Quest text

I noticed a few rocks that seemed unusual in the Dustbowl. Upon closer inspection, I saw that they had carvings! I could not resist making rubbings of them to study later. The nearby ogres took notice of my presence and before I could even react, I woke up under the care of the innkeeper, and with a terrible headache. Now, I am down all of my supplies and without any study material while I heal. Can you venture to the east in search of my pack, and take rubbings of the etchings yourself?
