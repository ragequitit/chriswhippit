In Demand - Plague Records

Quest · Booty Bay · Horde & Alliance
Level ~50Required –

Quest text

A group of historians contracted me to recover records of the great plague. Prove who's guilty and who's not or some such. Acquire a few of these records for me, and I'll reward you generously. Retrieve the Headmaster's Records from Darkmaster Gandling in the Scholomance, Grain Shipment Orders from Baron Rivendare in the ever-burning city of Stratholme, and Dathrohan's Correspondence from Grand Crusader Saidan Dathrohan in the Scarlet Bastion of Stratholme.
