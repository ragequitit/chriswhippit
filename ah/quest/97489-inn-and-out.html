Inn and Out

Quest · Searing Gorge · Alliance
Level ~50Required –XP 4400Money 78s 50c

Quest text

I overheard the brotherhood discussing a slave mining operation beneath what they call the Cauldron. It sounds like they aren't focused on liberating them as much as they are with disrupting their leadership. Now, I've spent a long time running inns and taverns, but never have I been in a position to help someone who couldn't at least leg it to my establishment. This seems like an opportunity to break in some freshly enchanted hearthstones. Who knows, maybe they'll make for good recruits?
