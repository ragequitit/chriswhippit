Stronger than Steel

Quest · Mulgore · Horde
Level ~8Required –XP 1750

Quest text

Have you heard what befell the burial ground at Gloomrise? Giant spiders--rumored to have a venom so strong that it can wake the dead--have spread like wildfire. Nasty business, if you ask me. One of the scouts dispatched to investigate returned half dead and covered in an incredibly resilient, crystalline spider silk. I harvested what I could, but I would highly value more samples of this silk for additional study. Bring me more of this crystalline silk and I will reward you.
