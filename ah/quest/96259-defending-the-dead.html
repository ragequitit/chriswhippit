Defending the Dead

Quest · Mulgore · Horde
Level ~8Required –XP 2350

Quest text

There is an ancient tauren burial ground at the place called Gloomrise, just northwest of this camp. Of late it has been infested with a particularly vicious brood of spiders. These spiders not only pose a threat to the people of Mulgore, they are also damaging a site that most tauren consider to be sacred. It would be of great service if you could travel to Gloomrise and put an end to this infestation. The ancestors will surely smile upon you if you see this done.
