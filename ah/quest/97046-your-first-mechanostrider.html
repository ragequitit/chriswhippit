Your First Mechanostrider

Quest · Dun Morogh · Alliance
Level ~9Required –XP 1550

Quest text

Salutations! I see today is a big day indeed; today you will synchronize your biometrics to the rider detection protocol of your first mechanostrider! This protocol will help the strider distinguish you from other possible riders. As such, you have an important choice to make. You and your strider will traverse the world together so choose wisely! Fear not, whatever choice you make will be the correct one. Go examine the mechanostriders and return to me when you have made your choice.
