The Elite of the Elite

Quest · Dustwallow Marsh · Horde · Elite
Level ~37Required –XP 1300

Quest text

A small force of Kor'kron soldiers passed through here a few days ago. Never told me why they were here, and I didn't ask. They bought supplies, took a bit of rest, then moved on. The Kor'kron are the elite of the elite and tougher than elekk hooves, but I can't help feeling that something went wrong. I did overhear one of them mention "the island", and there's an island called Alcaz just off the coast. It's a really nasty place. Get some friends and look into what befell these Kor'kron.
