Pilfered Windstones

Quest text

We are relying on windstones more and more for day-to-day living here on Zephras so we try and maintain a cache of them in reserve for emergencies. Lately I've noticed some go missing from our stores. We've found windstones on bandits we've captured recently, and I suspect they are behind our shortages. If you find yourself near the bandit cave to the north, mind knocking a few heads together and reclaiming some of our pilfered windstones for me?
