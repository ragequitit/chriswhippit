Objective: Find the Prayer to Elune and take it to Astarii Starseeker in Darnassus.$B

Will you assist me, mortal? Will you find the prayer to Elune? As I stated, the prayer was etched onto a golden tablet. One of these idol worshipping scoundrels must have it on their person. If you do recover my prayer, take it to Astarii Starseeker in Darnassus.
