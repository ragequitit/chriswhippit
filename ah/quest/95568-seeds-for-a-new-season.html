Quest text

You have arrived on Mount Hyjal at a time of changes, stranger. You may have seen that the landscape is scarred by the old war and tainted by fel energies. For Hyjal to survive, nature must bloom again. If you are willing to help me gather seeds from some of the untainted plants, I would be grateful. Many flowers grow in the wild lands east of here. Look for small red or yellow flowers.
