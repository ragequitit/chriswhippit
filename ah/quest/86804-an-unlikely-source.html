An Unlikely Source

Quest · Azshara · Horde & Alliance
Level ~56Required –XP 3950Money 83s 94c

Quest text

Furze is not the first with his affliction. Since the Legashi's attacks against us began, many Blackmaw have presented with a similar illness. Each one eventually fell to corruption, becoming mindless and destructive. I have been working towards a cure. My studies indicate that one could possibly be made by transmuting the Legashi's own firewater, but I cannot acquire it myself. You might find firewater, or a way to brew it, in the Legashi camps to the east. You would be a great help in doing so.
