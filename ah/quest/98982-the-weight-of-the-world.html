The Weight of the World

Quest · Mount Hyjal · Horde & Alliance
Level ?Required –

Quest text

You wade through toil and torment in search of riches and glory, ever pushing onward and upward. The weight of such an existence weighs heavily on all of us who choose this life. Should we not unburden our enemies of that which weighs heavily on their shoulders? In the city Stratholme reside two great, but opposing evils. Bring me the mantle of the false crusader, Dathrohan in his great bastion, and the pauldron of the one called Rivendare in his slaughterhouse sanctuary.
