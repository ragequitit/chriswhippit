Wrangling Apples

Quest · New Avalon · Horde & Alliance · Elite
Level ?Required –

Quest text

If you ask me, fresh fruit is the highlight of any meal. Apples are my favorite, and conveniently we have a thriving orchard south of here. Assuming it hasn't caught fire yet - which is a distinct possibility - I'll bet it's about time for a harvest! Normally the farmhands are willing to cut me a discount, but I still owe them a pie. Getting them for cheap is out of the question, but you look like you can afford them.
