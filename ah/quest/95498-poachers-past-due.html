Poachers Past Due

Quest · Burning Steppes · Horde & Alliance
Level ~54Required –XP 725Money 90s

Quest text

A while back I had a run-in with some of those shadowsilk scoundrels. Got the bullies off my back by sacrificing what coin I had on me, but they stole what was mine! You might not want to travel alone like I did though... they stick together! I still recall the names they called each other in the ambush. Ranthor, Bonzo, and Liu. Put an end to 'em and you can have what you want from their belongings.
