Forgotten Loa Idols

Quest · Durotar · Horde
Level ~9Required –XP 775

Quest text

When we fled the isles that Zalazane and his ilk now inhabit, we left behind many precious idols that help us commune with spirits we worship: our loa. They are of grave importance to our tribe. The followers of Zalazane show no reverence for our loa and have scattered these idols across the largest of the isles. This is an action that will not go unnoticed if we are not able to collect these idols, and offending a loa is a grave matter. Go, find as many loa idols as you can and return them to me.

Rewards

  • Depleted Ritual Band
