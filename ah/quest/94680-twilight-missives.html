Quest text

There have always been small pockets of Twilight's Hammer cultists here and there, but never have I seen them in such numbers. They seem emboldened by someone, or something, to expand their influence. I would know more of these cultists and what they seek. Bring me anything you find off their corpses that we may glean knowledge into their campaign.
