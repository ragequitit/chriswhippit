Paragons of Power: The Demoniac's Robes

Quest · Zul'Gurub · Horde & Alliance
Level ~60Required –XP 725

Quest text

The ultimate symbol of power amongst the tribe is the garb we wear in battle. A demoniac's robes have fused fine runecloth and devastatingly powerful mojo into a single fabric. The visage of the robe is said to instill fear into the minds of all who see it... especially the demons that the demoniac will eventually dominate. From Zul'Gurub, you must bring me a very specific set of the Paragons of Power. It will be difficult... but your success will assure you of acquiring a robe for yourself.
