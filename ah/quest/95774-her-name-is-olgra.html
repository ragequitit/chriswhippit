Quest text

The healer who saved me could not save her, and she has died in battle. Her name is Olgra, and I will never forget her. You did not find her adornments on her body? She wore many jewels of polished bone. Slay the Razormane Raiders near where she fell, and find her precious works of art! I will place them with the memorial that I build in her memory.
