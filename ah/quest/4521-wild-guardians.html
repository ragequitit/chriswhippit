Quest text

Objective: Trull Failbane in Felwood wants you to kill 15 Raging Owlbeasts and 15 Ragged Owlbeasts.

Winterspring lies just beyond Timbermaw, northeast of Felwood. Have you been there? I have heard that the area is laden with creatures called wildkin. These beasts have been known to guard secrets; secrets of the Night elves. We must figure out what may be hidden in Winterspring. I have heard the ragged owlbeasts can be found just south of the road, and that there are others called raging owlbeasts that may be found in the center of Winterspring. Clear them out, and gain access to the land.
