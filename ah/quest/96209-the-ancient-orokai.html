The Ancient, Orokai

Quest · Nighthaven · Horde & Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 625

Quest text

The ancient, Orokai, stands vigil just outside of town. Take this to him. The ancients are wondrous and wise. Provided this branch has not been separated from its parent tree for too long, he will be able to "speak" with it to learn what tales it has to tell. You can find Orokai to the southwest of the town, not too far away.
