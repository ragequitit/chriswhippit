Deliver the Signet

Quest text

Since you are going to Valanaar anyway, please deliver the signet ring you recovered from the tower to Talaanis Shadowsong in Valanaar. He will want this back, and more importantly, he will want to learn the fate of his nephew. Talaanis is the High Elder of Zephras Isle and more importantly, he is a good man who is well loved by the people, despite our current hardships. It pains me to think of how he will react to this news. Please express my condolences for his loss.
