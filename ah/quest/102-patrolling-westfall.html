Quest text

Objective: Bring 8 Gnoll Paws to Captain Danuvin on Sentinel Hill.

Stormwind has abandoned us. A foul wind of depravity rustles through the plains of Westfall. This was my homeland and I will not turn my back on the citizens who choose to remain here. We, the former farmers, shall make our stand. Your task, should you choose to accept, is to patrol the grasslands of Westfall. Track down and slay the vile Gnolls that seem to be working in conjunction with the Deadmines thieves. Bring me eight Gnoll Paws and I will reward your bravery.
