The Wrecked Caravan

Quest · Eastern Plaguelands · Horde & Alliance
Level ~60Required –XP 725Money 2g 20s 50c

Quest text

Lord Tyrosus has asked me to investigate the remnants of a Scarlet Crusade caravan in Plaguewood along the road to Stratholme. Recent reports tell of extensive crusade movements all across the plaguelands leading up to the schism at Tyr's Hand, and this caravan may be able to provide us with some indication as to their purpose. I'll meet you there and we will investigate this together.
