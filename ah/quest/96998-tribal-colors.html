Quest text

The skeleton still clutching a throwing spear has the telltale signs of a troll headhunter. In one of its bony clutches is a wrap of leather adorned with troll characters and smudges of face tints in Darkspear colors. This must have been a makeup pouch used to apply war paint. Darkspear tribe members may recognize the writing on the sacred skins.
