Barkbound Blessings

Quest text

You wish to learn from us? The trees of Hyjal speak to us. Their bark holds life, and on it grows the moss we call Moonveil. It is soft, green, and strong with the mountain's soul. We need this moss to heal our hurt and bless roots. Go, gentle one. Seek the old trees where the mist clings and the sun hides. Take Moonveil moss from their bark and bring it back to me. With it, we will mend what pains us.
