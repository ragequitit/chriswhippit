Paragons of Power: The Predator's Bracers

Quest · Zul'Gurub · Horde & Alliance
Level ~60Required –XP 725

Quest text

In the dark times after the fall of the Gurubashi Empire, Zandalar predators used their prowess to provide sustenance for the tribe. Hunters claim to be much like our tribe's predators - to seek the hunt is to embody the essence of the predator, yes. Your hunt in Zul'Gurub, however, will show us how you truly rate amongst our finest predators. Bring to me a sampling of the Paragons of Power from within Zul'Gurub. Do so, and you'll take first steps in becoming the penultimate predator.
