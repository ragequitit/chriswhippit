Quest · Riverglades · Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 1550

Quest text

If there is one thing a fighting force always needs, it's bandages. Unfortunately, Farholde has no reliable way to manufacture cloth. Thus, we depend on imports from the goblins or, if we are lucky, what we can take back from the orcs. Currently, our supply of quality cloth is running low. I will have to start making bandages of old shirts before long. If you have the means to aid us, you would be saving many lives.
