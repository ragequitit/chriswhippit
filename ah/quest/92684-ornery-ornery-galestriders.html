Quest · Zephras Isle · Horde & Alliance

Quest text

As if the harvest wasn't light enough this year already, the galestriders in the area have been getting into the orchard and stealing my stormapples. Think you can head out to the fields and cull them for me? Take care though stranger, these lowlands galestriders are bigger and angrier than the highlands variety. Watch yourself. A well-timed kick is all it takes to put you down for good if you aren't paying attention. Bring me some of their meat and I can make us a hot meal with it as well.
