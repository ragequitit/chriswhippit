Final Death

Quest · Mount Hyjal · Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 6400

Quest text

We've tried to escape the mountain, but our attempts have always been thwarted by the Scourge. A powerful lich reanimates the remains of those we destroy and sends them against us again. So long as that lich endures, we'll never be safe. The lich has a pile of bones from which it rebuilds skeletons. Take this torch and set fire to the bone pile in the ruins. The lich will be sure to respond. Bring me its amulet, so that we may destroy it and be rid of the lich for good.
