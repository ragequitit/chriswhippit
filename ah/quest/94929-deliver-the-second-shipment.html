Deliver the Second Shipment

Quest · Swamp of Sorrows · Alliance
Level ~43Required –XP 290

Quest text

I know I said that was it, but I promise, I wouldn't ask this if it weren't important. It's a shipment of utmost urgency. An old acquaintance resides in a keep within the Riverglades. To get there, travel northeast through the bogs avoiding the deadly creatures within. Along the northeastern hillside sits an old path. Travel its road all the way through the hills and when you hit the fork in the road stay north, then east. You should see Farholde along the eastern shore. I thank you for your aid.
