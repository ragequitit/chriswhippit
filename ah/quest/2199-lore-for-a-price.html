Quest text

Objective: Bring five silver bars to Talvash del Kissel in Ironforge.

I am a jeweler of great renown here in Ironforge! Well, former renown anyway... I have fallen onto hard times due to the chicanery of a competitor! A bad reputation in this business spells financial disaster. As a result, I find myself without a copper to my good-though-currently-sullied name. I want you to get me five silver bars to finish an order I received - the first in what seems like an eternity. You get me those five silver bars, and I'll answer your questions on the necklace.
