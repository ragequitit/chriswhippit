Remnants of War

Quest · Tirisfal Glades · Horde
Level ~9Required –XP 900

Quest text

It wasn't long ago that the citadel of Naxxramas bore down upon this city, intent on wiping the Forsaken off the map. Both the Forsaken and the Argent Dawn lost much that day to secure the future of the Forsaken. During the battle, the great necrotic focusing crystal mounted atop Naxxramas was shattered and its fragments were scattered all over this area. I suspect that is what the cultists are here for. Help me recover the fragments and deprive the Scourge of them.
