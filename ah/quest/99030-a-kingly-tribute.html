A Kingly Tribute

Quest · Mount Hyjal · Horde & Alliance
Level ?Required –

Quest text

In the ruins of Dire Maul lies the seat of the king of the Gordok ogres. This warlord has plundered most of the riches of the fallen kingdom of the kaldorei, including the crown jewels of the ruling house of ancient Eldre'Thalas. I require these gems and you will retrieve them for me. These riches will be well hidden, so you may need to use guile to not only defeat the king, but earn the tribute of his followers. Return the jewels to me and I will purify the corrupted leg armor you carry.
