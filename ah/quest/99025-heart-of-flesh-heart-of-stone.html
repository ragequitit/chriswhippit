Heart of Flesh, Heart of Stone

Level ?Required –

Quest text

There's power in a creature's heart. It's the core of one's life, and without it, the living cease to live. I sometimes miss the beating of my own heart, though I acknowledge that requiring a pulse to live is... strictly a liability. Travel to the Shaper's Terrace overlooking Un'goro Crater and retrieve the still-beating heart of the great Direhorn Matriarch there, as well as the hardened heart of the watcher, Nanaya. Do this and I will cleanse the armor piece you carry.
