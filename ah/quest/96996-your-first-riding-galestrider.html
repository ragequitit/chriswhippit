Quest text

Now that you have completed your training, it would seem the winds wish you to bond with your first strider. These galestriders have been ridden by our ancestors for generations. Despite their wispy and slender appearance, they are steadfast and fierce mounts. You and your strider will traverse the world together, so you have an important choice to make. Fear not, whatever choice you make will be the right one. Go examine the striders and return to me when you have made your choice.
