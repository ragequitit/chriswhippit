The Culling

Quest · Eastern Plaguelands · Horde & Alliance
Level ~60Required –XP 725Money 2g 88s

Quest text

The city behind us is a haunted place. I spent years here after my original death as a thrall of the Lich King yet even in my servitude, I maintained some amount of awareness. Countless times, I shambled by the body of my wife, frozen forever in the fear she felt when the end came. Please make your way through gate leading to the area now known as Slaughter Square. I will meet you there when I find my resolve. You will need allies for this.
