Bait the Beast

Quest · Mount Hyjal · Horde & Alliance · Elite
Level ?Required –XP 925

Quest text

The most vicious fel chimaera of Hyjal is known as Ghansurok. It likes to swoop in and prey on wildlife caught out of their burrows. Not only that, it's making hippogryph travel in and out of Hyjal more stressful than usual! First, hunt down one of the Nightsabers hiding in the Summit's shadows. Next, place meat from the fallen cat somewhere on the northern ridge where chimaeras feed. Lure Ghansurok towards it. Slay the beast and return with its heart. Marn will study it to help heal Hyjal.
