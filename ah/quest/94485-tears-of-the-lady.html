Tears of the Lady

Quest · Zephras Isle · Horde & Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 230

Quest text

If we are to save my fellows in the Nightclaw Den, I am going to need some ingredients for a poultice. The first and most important is a spindly moss called Lady's Tear. It typically grows on the base of the trees here in Shadowgale. This moss secretes a sticky substance that acts as an antiseptic and is perfect for binding open wounds. I have gathered what little I can reach but I know there is more in this forest. Go and be swift. We are going to need a lot if the injured I left behind still live.
