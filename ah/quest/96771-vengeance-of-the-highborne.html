Quest text

In the region known as the Riverglades to the southeast, there is a place called the Krol'dok Stronghold. A highborne known as Zalfan Moonshadow has been seen there in the company of the Twilight's Hammer. I'd pay handsomely if you'd rid the world of this cretin for me. He brings shame to the proud lineage of the highborne by consorting with fanatics and using stolen arcane knowledge in their service. There are few enough of us left without worms like him sullying our good name.
