Thunderhorn's Report

Quest · Mulgore · Horde
Level ~8Required –XP 85

Quest text

Our elders in Thunder Bluff must know of the progress we have made here, thanks to your aid. Please, take this to Arch Druid Hamuul Runetotem in Thunder Bluff.
