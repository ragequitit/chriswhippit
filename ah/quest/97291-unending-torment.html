Unending Torment

Quest · Ruins of Lordaeron · Horde
Level ?Required –XP 1650Money 13s

Quest text

With the head reattached, all we need now is to bind it to the Dark Lady's will. This should not be a problem. The beast barely has brains left as it is after all the beatings it's taken. We simply need to motivate it. Fetch me a Toxic Skullcap from Miss Grisette in the Trade Quarter. Oh, and some Blisterweed from our local herbalist trainers, the Alliestars. Feel free to just take some, they owe me. And lastly a bit of Essence of Agony. Any old poison vendor should do. We have a potion to make!
