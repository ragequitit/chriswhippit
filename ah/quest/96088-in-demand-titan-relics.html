In Demand - Titan Relics

Quest · Booty Bay · Horde & Alliance
Level ~50Required –

Quest text

Some researchers from Dalaran are looking for relics from the Titans and their followers, as well as their enemies in the Burning Legion. The relics are located in some tricky spots I can't reach. Recover them for me and I'll reward you generously. Retrieve Ysera's Tear from the Shade of Eranikus in the Temple of Atal'Hakkar, a Mark of Xavius from Varrox Bleakhoof in Blackmaw Hold, and Freya's Sigil from Nanaya in the Shaper's Terrace.

Rewards
