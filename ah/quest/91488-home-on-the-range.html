Home on the Range

Quest · Azshara · Horde
Level ~56Required –XP 4400Money 66s

Quest text

During our last skirmish with the blood elves, my tent was badly damaged. For three nights, I have been at the mercy of the elements. It can be repaired with a few mosshoof hides, but a lack of sleep has left me unable to hunt them myself. The easiest mosshooves to hunt are west and south of here, but the hide of any mooshoof will do.
