The Malfunctioning Construct

Quest · Zephras Isle · Horde & Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 90

Quest text

The construct before you buzzes and spurts and is clearly not functioning correctly. Drawing on your limited experience as an engineer, you suspect that it needs some form of repairs. There are a few Artisans in Valanaar that have some experience servicing cyclone constructs such as this one, and the hermit who taught you engineering may also have some insight.
