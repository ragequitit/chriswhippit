Stolen Saws

Quest · Riverglades · Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 2850

Quest text

The boys up at Southern Watch do a good job keeping gnolls out for the most part. Every now and then a few sneak into the camp at night and cause a bit of trouble. They especially like to steal saw blades, which they likely use to make weapons out of the sharp metal. If you could get some back for us, it'd be a big help. The gnolls have probably got 'em stashed away somewhere in their camps up in the hills to the west.
