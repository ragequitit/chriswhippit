Chol'aruk the Ravener

Quest · The Barrens · Alliance · Elite
Level ~18Required –XP 4100Money 50s

Quest text

There are rumors of a colossal abomination that makes its lair at a cave to the northwest of here, on the plateau known as Thorn Hill. The Razormane call it Chol'aruk or "The Ravener" and those that have seen it say it's so massive that it can swallow an orc whole. Few believe such tales, but I do... because I've fought one like it myself. On the honor of those I lost to these things, this beast must die. Gather strong allies and find the lair of Chol'aruk. Kill the abomination and bring me its head.
