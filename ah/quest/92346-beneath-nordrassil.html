Beneath Nordrassil

Quest · Azshara · Horde
Level ~56Required –XP 550

Quest text

You may have noticed the furbolg and satyr northeast of here, fighting over some burrow. The conflict does not interest me as much as what's inside. That burrow seems to lead beneath the world tree, and who knows what could be under there? We're spread too thin to investigate ourselves, but I know a tauren who may appreciate the tip. Her name is Nara Wildmane, a druid in Thunder Bluff. Tell her Jediga sent you. Always good to have someone owe you a favor.
