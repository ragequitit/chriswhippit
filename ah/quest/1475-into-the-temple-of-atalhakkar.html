Maintained by a vicious tribe of trolls named the Atal'ai, the temple is rumored to be impenetrable. Now sunk beneath the Pool of Tears, retrieving artifacts from the temple is even more difficult. But that's what I need you to do, <name>. Aid me by gathering the intact Atal'ai Tablets. Bring them to me and I will return to the Explorers' Guild a hero. Together we will share the glory.
