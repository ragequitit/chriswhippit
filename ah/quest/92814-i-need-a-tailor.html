I Need a Tailor

Quest · Desolace · Horde & Alliance
Level ~35Required –XP 2050Money 18s

Quest text

My mate, Halea, is tasked with ensuring our armaments are empowered with appropriate magical energy. The whole concept of enchanting is far beyond my understanding, but as the centaur whose job is to keep our village well supplied and equipped, I know that Halea is often behind on her duties--to no fault of her own. I would like to gift her some simple clothes to practice her craft on. I see some adventurers wandering through sandstorms with simple facemasks. Could you get me a few of those?
