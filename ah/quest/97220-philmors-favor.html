Philmor's Favor

Quest · Stormwind City · Alliance
Level ~33Required –XP 550

Quest text

Every day this week we've been short dockhands. Care to ease the stress on our cargo schedule? Take this package to the stockroom entrance of the Gatehouse. You'll find it inside Trias' Cheese shop on the second floor. The mistress, Elaine, will be quite familiar with the deliveries. The Gatehouse often has shipments arriving with more discretion to allow for ceremonial surprises on behalf of the guard's officers.
