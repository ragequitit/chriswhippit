Quest text

When the Legashi overran the Blackmaw, the rest of the Timbermaw sealed their passage to the hold to keep out the satyr. We had no idea they lived on as refugees. I would gladly aid my brethren, but we are fighting our own battles. Like the Legashi threaten the Blackmaw, the Deadwood do likewise to us. We haven't many warriors to spare. The Deadwood chieftain, Bloodmaw, is in Felpaw Village to the south. Eliminating him would greatly ease our struggle. Bring me his mace, and you will have our aid.
