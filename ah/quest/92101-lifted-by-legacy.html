Quest text

Hyjal is difficult to bear. Nightmares and murderous memories linger among the craters. But you carry warmth from Kalimdor within you! My friend Tobin also enlisted and joined up with me, but I fear the worst for his soul. The Alliance company gave us steeds to break in and bond with for our upcoming deployment. We took them on a joy ride through the Barrens. Carved our names into the bottom of the Great Lift in Thousand Needles. See if the marks remain? It will bring ease to a weary soul.
