Call of Earth

Quest · Zephras Isle · Horde & Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 270

Quest text

While we owe much of our prosperity to the elements of wind, the path we walk requires that we obey the call of all four of the primary elements and undertake a trial for each. Before you begin your first trial in earnest however, you must prove the strength of your resolve. Travel to the standing stones to the east. A force of Al'Aketh converts has been spotted there and they must be driven off. Confront the cultists there and bring me one of their signets as proof of your deeds.
