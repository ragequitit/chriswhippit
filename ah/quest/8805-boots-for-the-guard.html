    Objective: Bring 3 Ornate Mithril Boots to Vish Kozus, Captain of the Guard at Cenarion Hold in Silithus. You must also bring Logistics Task Briefing II in order to complete this quest.

    The mounted division of the Cenarion Hold guard is in short supply of ornate mithril boots. Procure a batch and deliver them immediately to Captain Vish Kozus in Cenarion Hold.
