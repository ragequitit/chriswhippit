Quest text

The Skullsplitter fetishes Brother Nimetz sent with you are indeed powerful, though I do not think they contributed to Kurzen's condition, or at least not directly. Still, if the rumors are true and the lost troll city of I'lalai has indeed risen from its watery grave, it is possible the dark magics used to raise the doomed city may be related.
