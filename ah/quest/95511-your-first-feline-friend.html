Your First Feline Friend

Quest · Darnassus · Alliance
Level ~53Required –XP 1550

Quest text

Greetings, friend! I see today is a big day indeed; today you will bond with your first saber. As you may or may not know these sabers are fiercely intelligent and extremely loyal to their bonded rider. You won't find a more noble and steadfast companion. As such, you have an important choice to make. You and your saber will traverse the world together, so choose wisely! Fear not, whatever choice you make will be the correct one. Go, examine the sabers and return to me when you have made your choice.
