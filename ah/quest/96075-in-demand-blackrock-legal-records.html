In Demand - Blackrock Legal Records

Quest · Booty Bay · Horde & Alliance
Level ~50Required –

Quest text

My employers are looking to get their hands on legal documents surrounding the ownership of Blackrock Mountain. Some kind of property dispute. In any case, if you could acquire these key records, I can afford to reward you generously. Retrieve Nefarian's Remit from General Drakkisath in the upper part of Blackrock Spire, an Overseer's License from Overlord Wyrmthalak in the lower part of Blackrock Spire, and a Bronzebeard Wedding Contract from Emperor Thaurissan in the Blackrock Depths.
