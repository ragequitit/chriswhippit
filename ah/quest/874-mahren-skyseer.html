Objective: Speak with Mahren Skyseer.

You have hunted the beasts of the Barrens. Your spirit is strong. But a hunter must always be prepared. A true hunter can stalk prey down any path. Mountains and swamps will not sway him. Not even the sea. Find my sister, Mahren. She hunts the great water beasts along the coast. She will be your teacher in their ways. But be wary in your search for her, for the Barrens' coast is held by humans.
