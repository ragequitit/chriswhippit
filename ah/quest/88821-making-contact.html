Making Contact

Quest · Azshara · Alliance
Level ~56Required –XP 490

Quest text

While scouting along the cliffs of the Shattered Strand, one of our sentinels noticed a kaldorei woman on the beach. Clearly, she is not with our expedition, and she did not have the makings of a fighter. I wonder then, what she could be doing in such a hostile place as this. If you can, try to make contact with her. I would like to know her purpose for being here, and if our interests are perhaps aligned. She was last seen on the eastern coast, along the north side of the Bay of Storms.
