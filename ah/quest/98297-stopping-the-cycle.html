I did a quick test flight while ye were out. The good news is the armor ye helped me craft for Bassbeak works like a charm! The very bad news is I saw a group o' them Dragonmaw in the process of enslavin' a red dragonspawn. Absolutely not. Not again. You have tae take it out. You'll certainly need friends tae do so.
