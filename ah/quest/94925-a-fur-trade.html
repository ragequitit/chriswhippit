A Fur Trade

Quest · Riverglades · Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 3000

Quest text

Farholde prides itself on its independence, but there are a few things we can only get through trade. We've negotiated an arrangement with a dealer at Powderfuse Port. We provide her with goods that are dangerous to acquire, and she provides us with specialty munitions. Currently, she's in the market for lion fur. A pride of them live atop a large hill in the center of the plains. If you'd like to make yourself useful, you could aid me in harvesting some of their hides.
