Objective: Bring Vek'nilash's Circlet, 2 Idols of Night, 5 Bronze Scarabs and 5 Ivory Scarabs to Andorgos in Ahn'Qiraj. You must also attain Friendly reputation with the Brood of Nozdormu to complete this quest.

In my visions I see their monstrous faces adorned by powerful crowns. But I will have my revenge... I will transmute these symbols of Qiraji power to ones better suited to our needs.
