Quest text

Objective: Retrieve Helcular's Rod from the Yeti and bring it back to Novice Thaivand in Tarren Mill.

Master Helcular was almost complete with his transformation into a Lich when a human lynch mob discovered his ritual lair in the hills and brutally killed him. To make matters worse they buried his corpse in the Southshore cemetery. Such disregard for the great wizard is unacceptable. As his apprentice I shall see to it that the rains of vengeance fall upon the town of Southshore. We will need Helcular's Rod first. Yeti overtook his ritual lair. Undoubtedly one of the beasts has it.
