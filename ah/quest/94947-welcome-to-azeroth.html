It's a big world down here, and we need our people to start branching out to explore it. That's where you come in. The Kirin Tor is a member of an "Alliance" of like-minded nations. The de-facto heart of this alliance is the city of Stormwind to the south. Elaadrin has asked me to find someone to travel to Stormwind and meet with the regent lord there, a human named Bolvar Fordragon. When you are ready, the Kirin Tor mages have set up a special portal for the High Order to use to access Stormwind.
