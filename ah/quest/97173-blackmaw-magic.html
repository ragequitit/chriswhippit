Quest text

The Blackmaw furbolgs in the mountains overlooking Azshara have been dabbling in dark magic. While a primitive people being corrupted by demonic magic is nothing new for Azeroth, Lan'dalock and I would like to study their spell foci. Travel to the stronghold known as Blackmaw Hold and recover one of the Blackmaw Ritual Totems for us. Only the most powerful of the Blackmaw's shamans will be carrying one of these, so bring allies and take care.
