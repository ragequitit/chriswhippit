The Bounty of Nature

Quest · Mount Hyjal · Horde & Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 4350

Quest text

You have done well and helped me, and the mountain, greatly. I am just preparing some stew, and I would be glad to share it if you would help me gather some of the ingredients. While I am loath to slay any among the wild animals, the cycle of life requires sustenance. A balance of ingredients would serve us well. A few Hyjal Venison Cuts from the Hyjal Stags would pair well with the starchy roots of the Scarlet Dart Tuber plants that grow in the green places nearby.

Rewards

  • Ketharas' Hyjal Stew
