Objective: Gather 6 Gooey Spider Legs and bring them to Chef Grual in Darkshire.

Bring me Gooey Spider Legs and I'll whip you up a few of them. I hear Venom Web Spiders are a good source - they nest to the north, between the foothills and the river.
