Bloom of the Heavens

Quest · Wetlands · Alliance
Level ~26Required –XP 1750

Quest text

I came to Menethil Harbor from Auberdine, from the great tree Teldrassil, to recover a bloom of great import to my people. My people's ship the Flying Osprey crashed here many moons ago, joined with the Kul Tiran Third Fleet. Among its cargo was the flower known as Nord'el--Bloom of the Heavens in your tongue--bloomed under a full moon at the peak of Mount Hyjal, our former home. Please help me retrieve it! The wreckage is just south of the city, but it is inhabited by fierce murloc raiders!

Rewards
