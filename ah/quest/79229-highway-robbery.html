Highway Robbery

Quest · Desolace · Horde & Alliance
Level ~35Required –XP 1550Money 25s

Quest text

The campsite before you is a grisly scene. Judging by the remains, the victims were two humans and a dwarf. Markings on the bones indicate a savage attack, but the looted supplies tell you this wasn't done by an animal. The wounds don't match centaur weaponry either. Perhaps an experienced scavenger of these lands would know what to make of this.
