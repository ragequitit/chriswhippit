Your First Horse

Quest · Hillsbrad Foothills · Horde & Alliance
Level ~32Required –

Quest text

Greetings! Are you here to examine our horses? These steeds are descendants from both the historical Lordaeron stock and our own local horses. You won't find a more steadfast companion for your journeys. Choosing one may be difficult as they are all excellent and trained to be steady even when in danger. Don't forget, you and your horse will traverse the world together. I'm sure whatever choice you make will suit you best. Go examine the horses and return to me when you have made your choice.
