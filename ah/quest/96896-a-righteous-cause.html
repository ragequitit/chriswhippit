A Righteous Cause

Quest · Tirisfal Glades · Horde
Level ~9Required –XP 90

Quest text

Danitha and I fought against the Scourge in the defense of the Undercity when Naxxramas attacked. We lost many good friends that day, including the hero after which this keep was named--Garek Bandarion. He might just have been the first Forsaken to rediscover how to wield the Light as a paladin. I am encouraged to see his legacy and potential live on here with Danitha and her cohort. If only the rest of the Forsaken felt the same way...
