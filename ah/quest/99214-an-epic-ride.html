Quest text

Greetings, friend! I am pleased to see you again. Today you have enough experience and aptitude to bond with our fastest and fiercest sabers. These sabers are, of course, extremely intelligent and fiercely loyal to their bonded rider. You won't find a more noble and steadfast companion. As such, you have an important choice to make. You and your saber will traverse the world together, so choose wisely! Go, examine the armored sabers and return to me when you have made your choice.
