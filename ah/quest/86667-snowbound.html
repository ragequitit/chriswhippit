Snowbound

Quest · Loch Modan · Alliance
Level ~15Required –XP 420

Quest text

My old mentor, Norric, sent a minor elemental with an odd request. He needs a jar of snow for some of his shamanic workings! I don't know what he's going to do with that, but I learned a long time ago not to question his ways with respect to magic. I'm busy learning about the local elementals, so I can't leave to help him. Go to the tunnel entrance at South Gate Pass where there's snow year-round. Scoop some up in this ceramic jar and bring it to him before it melts. The jar should keep it cold.
