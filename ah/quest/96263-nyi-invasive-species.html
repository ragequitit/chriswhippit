NYI Invasive Species

Quest · Mulgore · Horde
Level ~8Required –

Quest text

The mounts of the Skyborne are known as galestriders, and they are beautiful creatures. Unfortunately, no sooner had the Skyborne arrived than several of the large birds broke free and flee across the plateau. As they are not native to Kalimdor and breed quickly, care must be taken lest they damage the ecosystem here. I need you to take this leash and capture the ones that escaped before they gain a foothold and begin multiplying. Return any you find to their handler near the skycutter dock. {race}
