Quest text

The ruin known as Dire Maul is a solemn tomb. Countless kaldorei souls have died to pay the balance of a grim bargain struck with an unthinking, uncaring evil. All in the name of maintaining the splendor and immortality of a privileged few. Go there and slay the beast known as Immol'thar that lies at the center of the western wing of the city. Bring me a phial of its cursed blood and break the cycle of death in that haunted place. Then I will cleanse the artifact you carry.
