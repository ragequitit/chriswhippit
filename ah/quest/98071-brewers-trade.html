Brewer's Trade

Quest · Hillsbrad Foothills · Alliance
Level ~32Required –XP 2200

Quest text

Worked a bit o' a trade with the Dun Garok brew-kin. We're not the only ones missin' the vineyards of the Fallrook family, it seems! Most o' the best bottled years are long since drank, but we do have cases of the wine still in the cellars. Bring this case of Fallrook Varietal to Dun Garok in the southeast and leave it with 'em by their gatherin' fireplace at the bottom of their fort. In return, carry a sack o' the Homebrew Hops back with ye. That keep is the closest thing to home around here!
