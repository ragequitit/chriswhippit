Quest text

When my meandering brought me here you can imagine my surprise to find my son alive and well. He did not reject me as the other living did, he embraced me. He had become a healer, and was studying the undead. He was convinced that he could find a cure to not only prevent undeath, but reverse it. For a short time, we were together and happy. I helped my son with his research, and he gave me the hope I needed. That is, until Baelin Caldoran rode back into our lives.
