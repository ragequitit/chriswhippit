Flutterfly Dust

Quest · Zephras Isle · Horde & Alliance
Level ?Required –XP 600

Quest text

I'm sure you've noticed the giant flutterflies out in the outer fields near the orchard? They are pleasant to look at, but they also have other benefits; namely their wing dust. It can be a mess, but if you can gather it up in large amounts, it has useful household applications. Take this Flutterfly Swatter and give the big bugs a good swat for me. They'll usually just get scared and flee, but sometimes they'll take some offense. Either way, they'll give their rumps a shake and drop a pile of dust.

Rewards

  • Flutterfly Swatter
  • Minor Mana Potion
