Truly, this is a fascinating object. It shows indications of being much more than a mere carving or sculpture. Seek out Muln Earthfury, a direct approach is fitting for the gravity of what you carry. You can find him and other elders of the Earthen Ring council at the Skywatcher Plateau in northwest Mulgore.
