The Return of the King?

Quest · Dustwallow Marsh · Alliance

Quest text

Take this. It's a handy bit o' glamour that the mages back home put together to make a quick disguise. It'll make you look like a Kul Tiras Marine which should do nicely for this mission. Sneak in to the tower and do yer best to listen for any mention of the man that washed up on the beach. I warn you though, do not get too close to Jaina. She will see right through this disguise. Come back and see me if you lose your disguise, I'll set you up with a new one.
