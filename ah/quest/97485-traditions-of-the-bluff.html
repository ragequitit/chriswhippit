Traditions of the Bluff

Quest · Thunder Bluff · Horde
Level ~47Required –XP 420Money 1s 75c

Quest text

Blessings of An'she, friend. We tauren have a tradition of scattering incense to the winds and I was hoping you'd be interested in honoring this tradition. You will need to craft the incense by purchasing herbs from Nida, cedar from Nata, sinew thread from Mahu in the mid rise, and ceremonial flint and tinder from Naal on the upper rise. Once you have these items, combine them and spread them off the south facing cliff of Hunter's Rise. You will find a mat and incense at the ceremonial site.

Rewards

  • Prepared Incense
