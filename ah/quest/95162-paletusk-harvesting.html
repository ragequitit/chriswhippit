Paletusk Harvesting

Quest · Riverglades · Horde
Level ?Required –XP 3300

Quest text

With so little land not claimed by the humans or Twilight's Hammer, this ranch is one of our very few sources of food. Ogres aren't much in the way of farmers, but they love a good paletusk roast, and have agreed to let me stay so long as the meat keeps coming. As our war advances, the demand for food is only ever increasing. Now I hardly have enough food to keep these ogres from looking at me like I'm their next meal. If we want to have enough meat for Rog'mar, I'll need your help with the harvest.
