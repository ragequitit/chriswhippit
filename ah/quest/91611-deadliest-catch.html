Deadliest Catch

Quest · Azshara · Horde · Elite
Level ~56Required –XP 5800

Quest text

If you wish for a true test of angling prowess, there are bigger fish than you can catch with a rod. Mammoth Sharks lurk in the deepest waters of the northern and southern coasts. They swim far out, right before the sea floor drops away into the great ocean. They are massive creatures. I would advise you to bring a hunting party. If you do go after one, bring me a cut of the meat.
