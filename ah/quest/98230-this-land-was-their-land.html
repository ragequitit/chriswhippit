This Land Was Their Land

Quest · Wetlands · Alliance
Level ~26Required –XP 240

Quest text

After the War of the Three Hammers that tore dwarven life as we knew it apart, the Wildhammers settled here, founding the mountain fortress of Grim Batol. It was a wondrous place. Gryphons soared everywhere you looked, diving down through the wetlands to grab a fish. It didn't last long, sadly. Howin Kindfeather is one of the first Wildhammers to return in ages. Keeps to himself, far outside of town. Follow the road east--you'll find him eventually. He could surely use a hand.
