Twisted Hatred

Quest · Teldrassil · Alliance · Elite
Level ~9Required –XP 900

Quest text

Lord Melenas did not act alone; he was at the behest of another. The one you seek is Xethorr the Wicked. It seems he has twisted the spiders that call Teldrassil home, intending their venom for some foul plan. He has taken residence deep in the forest, in the area to the northwest of here known as the Cleft. Take caution, and bring allies, for Xethorr is not an opponent to be trifled with. Dispatch his brood, and bring me anything that may tell us more of the satyr's work here.
