Memories of Rockard

Quest · Blasted Lands · Horde
Level ~55Required –XP 5650

Quest text

This trinket, carried by the ogre, seems to be a small stone carving depicting an orc family. It may be a memento from the orcs who lived here when this was the town of Rockard, before the ogres invaded. It probably has sentimental or spiritual value to the survivors at Stonard.
