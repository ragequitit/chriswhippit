Now We're Cooking

Quest · Mount Hyjal · Horde
Level ?Required –XP 3000

Quest text

In addition to seeing to the spiritual needs of the people here, I feed them as well. I'm not exactly a trained cook, but after five years, I've learned a thing or two about preparing food! I'm sure that folks here would love a taste of something different. I remember, before we assembled on the mountain, there was a goblin from Everlook who was serving platters of tiny, Savory Stag Sliders... I would love to taste one of those again. We have plenty of stags up here, too! Think you can cook some up?
