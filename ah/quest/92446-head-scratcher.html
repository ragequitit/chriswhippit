Head Scratcher

Quest · Mount Hyjal · Horde · Elite
Level ?Required –XP 925

Quest text

South of here, among da forests where I sometimes hunt, dere is a great and cunning bear. We call him Head Scratcher, as his paws have caught more dan one unsuspecting villager. He ambushes hunters who are returning with prey, striking da heads by surprise and knocking dem unconscious, then stealing da catch and running back into da forest. He is wise to me, he knows dat I track him. But he may not know your scent yet. Take dis carcass and lure him out. Finish him and bring back his claws.
