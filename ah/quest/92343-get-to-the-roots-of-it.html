Get to the Roots of It

Quest text

The burrow you speak of is a tunnel to Blackmaw Hold. In the past, the furbolg have guarded it fiercely, making it inaccessible to outsiders. With this satyr situation, however, we can get inside. So close to Nordrassil, there would be actively growing roots. Older roots can be seen on the world's surface, but newer ones can only be found deep underground. Little is known of their magical properties. If you were to bring me some samples to study, the druids of Thunder Bluff would be in your debt.
