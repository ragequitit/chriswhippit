To Feed an Army

Quest · Searing Gorge · Alliance
Level ~50Required –XP 4400Money 83s 50c

Quest text

If I am to be keeping a stockpile of goods for adventurers passing through, I need ample supply of food. The Alliance can have their breads and cheese, but true soldiers of the Horde eat meat! Upon my arrival, I saw a pack of worgs wandering far to the southeast, on the other side of the ravine. Bring enough meat to fill a few crates. While you're out, I've heard the Alliance innkeeper muttering something about spiders as a delicacy. I'm skeptical but curious, so fetch a few to taste as well.
