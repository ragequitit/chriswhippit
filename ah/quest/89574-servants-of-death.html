Servants of Death

Quest · Eastern Plaguelands · Horde & Alliance
Level ~60Required –XP 725Money 2g 88s

Quest text

To me, there is nothing more low than the living agents of the lich king. I disdain and reject those with the free will to see right from wrong, yet who choose to willingly serve death. Take the plague samples once again and test them on the living Cult of the Damned members nearby. Do not pity these monsters nor show them mercy. They've thrown in their lot with the damned, so damned they shall be.
