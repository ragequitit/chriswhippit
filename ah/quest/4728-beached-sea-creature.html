Quest text

Objective: Take the Sea Creature Bones to Gwennyth Bly'Leggonde in Auberdine.

The corpse of what appears to be some sort of giant sea mammal lies partially gutted on the beach. A wheelbarrow and various tools lie abandoned by this enormous beached sea creature, as though an attempt to study it had been made previously. Though murlocs have scavenged this beast, enough of it remains to gather a suitable sample for Gwennyth Bly'Leggonde in Auberdine.
