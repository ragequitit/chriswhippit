    Quest text

    Objective: Report the Ship Schedules to an authority in Steamwheedle Port.

    This scroll contains the schedules of shipments between Steamwheedle Port and Booty Bay. Both past and future shipments are included, along with ship names, with large 'X's next to some ships' journeys, and annotations like "GREAT LOOT" and "HARD FIGHT" next to others. Someone at Steamwheedle Port would want to see this document.
