Quest text

To the Esteemed Head Chef of New Avalon: Carrie Hearthfire, I trust this letter finds you in good spirits! Your departure from Hearthglen has left a hole in our hearts, and in our stomachs. Not a day passes by without a complaint of the morning gruel, yearning for the next delightful meal. To that end we are planning a banquet; one which we intend to serve only the finest dishes in Lordaeron: yours. An escorted caravan will arrive in a fortnight. You and your meals ought best be prepared.
