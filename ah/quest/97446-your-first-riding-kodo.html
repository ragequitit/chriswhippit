Quest text

Ah, I've been expecting you {class}. It would seem the winds have brought you to me this day. You have come seeking a kodo, correct? The kodo is a noble beast that is slow to anger, and you would be hard pressed to find a more dependable companion. Today you have an important choice to make. You and your kodo will travel the world together, so find which one speaks to you. Fear not, whatever choice you make will be the right one. Go examine the kodos and return to me when you have made your choice.
