Quest text

Objective: Bring Ouro's Intact Hide, 2 Idols of Death, 5 Bronze Scarabs and 5 Ivory Scarabs to Kandrostrasz inside Ahn'Qiraj. You must also attain Friendly reputation with the Brood of Nozdormu to complete this quest.

You seek to prove your worth to my kind, mortal? Very well, perhaps we can be of mutual assistance. The ancient sandworm, Ouro, is a being of legendary power. He is rumored to have been created by the Old God himself as a mockery of life. His skin is unnatural... thick, nearly impregnable, yet it's flexible and allows him to move with the speed and grace of a much smaller creature. Bring me a sample of his skin along with other minor components and I shall craft you leggings of great power.
