Objective: Speak to Witch Doctor Mau'ari again in a moment to receive the lucky charm.

Now, before I give ya dis charm, I better explain how ta use it. If you carry da charm with ya, you'll find dat certain items will drop from creatures ya fight. If you don't have da charm with you, you won't find them. Understand? You'll know these special items by the name -- they be called E'ko. If you find any of these items, just bring dem to ol' Mau'ari, and she help ya out. Wait here while I fix your charm.
