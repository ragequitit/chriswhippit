Quest text

Objective: Bring Stormpike's Request to Furen Longbeard in Stormwind.

There is a dwarven armorsmith whose work I admire greatly. His name is Furen Longbeard, and his skill is unmatched. I need a new shield, and I must have one of his! The problem is... Furen is far to the south, in the dwarven district within Stormwind. In human lands! I can't imagine why he would live there, so far from Ironforge. The humans must pay him a king's ransom to keep him there! So, if you want to travel and don't mind making some money, then will you send Furen my request for a shield?
