Quest text

In its day the so-called Sunken Temple was home to a sect of trolls dedicated to the resurrection of their ancient blood deity, Hakkar the Soul-Flayer. Before the Atal'ai could realize their grim ambitions, Ysera's wrath smote the temple into the mire and she left the dragon Eranikus and his host as its guardians. Of late I have heard many whispers of trolls once again active there, and I fear what may have become of the dragons there. Please, venture inside the temple and see what you can learn.
